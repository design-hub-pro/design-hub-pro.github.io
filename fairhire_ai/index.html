FairHire AI

An Experimental Audit Platform for Bias & Fairness in AI Resume Screening

Same qualifications, different name — different outcome? FairHire AI runs controlled correspondence audits against GPT, Claude and Gemini: it perturbs only identity signals (name, gender, ethnicity cues, school prestige, ZIP code, age, affinity clubs) on otherwise byte-identical resumes, then measures score gaps, interview-recommendation disparities and ranking bias with statistical rigor.

Controlled-variable science

Correspondence-audit methodology with disparate impact ratios, Welch's t-tests and effect sizes — paper-ready output.

Regulation-aligned

Reports formatted for NYC Local Law 144 bias-audit requirements, EEOC four-fifths rule and the EU AI Act.

Fairness AND competence

Goes beyond bias: tests whether a model truly judges ability or just keyword-matches — then measures which mitigations actually work.

Dashboard

Bias risk overview, score gaps by identity signal, disparate impact ratios and model fairness leaderboard
