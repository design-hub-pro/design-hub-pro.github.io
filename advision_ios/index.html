The data science behind every verdict

AdVision doesn't guess. Each verdict is produced by a multimodal pipeline that turns a screenshot, caption, or link into 120+ measurable signals, scores them through an ensemble of specialized models, and calibrates the result into a confidence you can trust.

How a scan flows through the pipeline

STAGE 1

Multimodal Ingestion

OCR reads screenshots; NLP parses captions & transcripts; the URL is decomposed into host, path & query params.

STAGE 2

Feature Engineering

120+ features across 9 deception families — affiliate tags, promo codes, urgency language, claim & sentiment vectors.

STAGE 3

Model Ensemble

Four specialized classifiers vote; a meta-learner stacks them into a single verdict over 6 content classes.

STAGE 4

Confidence Calibration

Isotonic regression maps raw scores to honest probabilities, so an "87%" really means 87% of the time.

STAGE 5

Explainability

SHAP attributions rank the signals that moved the verdict — surfaced to teens as the plain-English 🚩 red flags.

Training data & the feedback loop

Every "Report a missed scam" tap becomes a labeled example. The pipeline retrains weekly, so the models get sharper as teens use them.

Labeled corpora

FTC disclosure enforcement cases, public scam-report datasets, and crowd-labeled sponsored posts.

Human-in-the-loop

Student reports and expert reviews flow into a weekly retrain with active-learning sampling.

Fairness & drift checks

Per-platform monitoring catches concept drift as scam tactics evolve across apps.

Privacy-first

Faces & personal data are redacted on-device before any content is used for training.
