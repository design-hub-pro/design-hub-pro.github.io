Growth Path

From Prototype to Platform

1

Build the Smart Phone Holder Prototype

Assemble the ESP32-based phone holder with weight sensors using off-the-shelf components (~$25). Create the basic mobile app with Pomodoro timer and phone dock detection. Test with a small group of 10-15 students at your school.

2

Develop AI Planning & Rerouting Engine

Integrate task decomposition algorithms and the adaptive rerouting system. Partner with your school's psychology department to study focus patterns. Collect behavioral data to train the distraction detection model.

3

Scale to Campus-Wide Pilot Program

Partner with university tutoring centers and academic support services. Apply for education technology grants (NSF, Gates Foundation). Develop a research paper on the effectiveness of closed-loop focus systems for student success.
