Spring Peeper

Pseudacris crucifer · Hylidae

A small chorus frog whose permeable skin absorbs whatever is in the water and air around it. The earliest spring caller in the Northeast. Two reasons it's an exceptional bioindicator:

Water-quality sensor

Eggs and tadpoles fail above nitrate >5 mg/L or pH <4.5. Population dips signal upstream pollution before chemistry tests flag it.

Phenology clock

First chorus is triggered by sustained ~5°C nights. Calling date has shifted 11 days earlier since 2008 — the cleanest local proxy for climate warming.
