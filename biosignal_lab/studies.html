URBANIZATION BIODIVERSITY METAANALYSIS

The 60% threshold: where biodiversity collapses in the Northeast Atlantic Corridor

Pooling 12 years of BioSignal field data across 142 sites with NLCD impervious surface and EPA AirNow, we identify a sharp non-linear inflection at ~60% built-cover where Shannon diversity drops from gradual decline to collapse.

Key finding

At ~60% impervious cover, the rate of species loss accelerates 3.4× — suggesting a habitat-fragmentation threshold. Below the threshold, restoration can yield outsized recovery.
