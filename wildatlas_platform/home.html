Black-throated Blue Warbler

Setophaga caerulescens

A migratory songbird returning to the Northeast each May. Males flash slate-blue backs and a sharp black throat; females wear olive-brown. Listen for a wheezy, rising "zur-zur-zur-ZREE" in canopy edges.
