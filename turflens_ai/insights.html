412 courses · 28 countries · 2020–2025
HEADLINE FINDING

Across 412 courses, maintained turf accounts for 48.7% of land area — and 71% of estimated water use.

A satellite-derived 5-class land-cover model reveals that arid-region courses run 2.7× more turf area than coastal links, while sandbelt courses retain the most natural habitat. The footprint differences across climate zones are larger than differences within a zone.
