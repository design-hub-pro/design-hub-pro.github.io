TurfLensAI
Satellite × Ecology · Research platform

Measuring the environmental footprint of every golf course on earth, from space.

TurfLens AI segments satellite imagery into 5 ecological classes — maintained turf, tree canopy, water, bare/pavement, and natural vegetation — then quantifies each course's habitat, water use, and ecology score. Built on Sentinel-2 and NAIP; powered by DeepLabV3+.
