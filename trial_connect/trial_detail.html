About This Trial

This study evaluates the efficacy and safety of a novel targeted therapy in patients with HER2-positive breast cancer. The treatment combines immunotherapy with precision medicine approaches to improve outcomes.

Eligibility Criteria

Age 18-75 years old
Confirmed HER2+ breast cancer diagnosis
ECOG performance status 0-1
No prior treatment with HER2-targeted agents
