The Problem

The Hidden Crisis of Persuasive Misinformation

Short-form video platforms have become primary news sources for youth, but not all misleading content is obviously false. Much of it appears credible through sophisticated narrative structures, emotional appeals, and selective data presentation—making it nearly impossible to distinguish from legitimate information.

The Urgency of Media Literacy Education

Health Misinformation Epidemic

Nearly half of health-related TikTok videos analyzed contained non-factual information, with many coming from non-medical influencers presenting claims as expert advice.

University of Chicago Study

Youth Overconfidence Gap

Research shows young people express high confidence in detecting misinformation, yet struggle to actually identify false content—often ignoring rather than fact-checking questionable claims.

ScienceDirect Research

Massive Platform Exposure

Average TikTok users spend 34 hours monthly on the platform, and 40% of Gen-Z now use it for searching information—yet top results frequently contain misinformation.

EU Misinformation Report

Demand for Education

Growth Path

From Prototype to Platform

1

School Pilot Program

Partner with 2-3 local high schools to test the app in media literacy classes. Gather feedback from students and teachers to refine the analysis features and learning modules.

2

Youth Organization Expansion

Expand to youth organizations like Boys & Girls Clubs and after-school programs. Create educator guides and workshop materials for facilitators teaching digital citizenship.

3

Open Platform & API

Release as open-source tool with API access for researchers and educators. Partner with fact-checking organizations and academic institutions for ongoing validation.

Your Journey

From Curiosity to Impact

How your interest in media and technology led you here

Foundation

"I grew up as a digital native, spending hours on TikTok and YouTube. I thought I could tell what was real and what wasn't—until I realized how wrong I was."

Observation

"I watched my younger sister believe a viral health claim that turned out to be completely made up. She's smart—it just looked so convincing. That's when I saw the real problem."

Insight

"What if AI could detect the patterns that make misleading content feel trustworthy? Not to tell people what to believe—but to help them see the techniques being used on them."

Innovation

"TrustLens AI isn't about being the truth police. It's about giving my generation the tools to think critically in a world that's trying to manipulate us every second we're online."
