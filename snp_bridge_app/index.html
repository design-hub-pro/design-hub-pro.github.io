BRIDGE is the companion app for Stanford's flagship neurodiversity program — guiding students through a 2-week strengths-based journey, powering real advocacy projects, and uniting students, parents, and team leaders in the world's largest neurodiversity community.

Build advocacy projects

Every participant ships a neurodiversity advocacy project for their school or community — tracked, mentored, and showcased to the world.
