Halfway

Rehearse the conversation before you have it, from whichever side you are on. Teenagers practise saying the thing. Parents practise hearing it, and find out what a one-word answer was actually carrying.
Two RolesAI RoleplayFamily CommunicationConsent by Design
Almost every parent-teen app is built for one side and sold to the other, which is why teenagers delete them. Halfway gives each side its own product and shares almost nothing between them. The parent learns that their child practised a hard conversation. Never a word of what was said.
Pick a side→ Teen: situation→practise→debrief  ·   Parent: translate→practise→debrief→ Same conversation, for real
