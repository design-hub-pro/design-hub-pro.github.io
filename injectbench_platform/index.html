InjectBench

An open benchmark for prompt-injection robustness across LLMs — and the defenses that stop them.

A reproducible research platform that stress-tests frontier language models against a curated corpus of injection and jailbreak attacks, then quantifies exactly how much each defense strategy reduces attack success under one controlled evaluation protocol.

1,240 adversarial prompts

Across 9 attack categories — from direct overrides to indirect RAG injection.

18 models, one protocol

Seed-averaged, auto-judged, and fully reproducible from a single CLI command.

12 defenses, measured

Attack-success reduction vs. utility cost vs. latency — no marketing, just numbers.
