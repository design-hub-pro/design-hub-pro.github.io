A reproducible benchmark for prompt-injection robustness across frontier LLMs.

InjectBench stress-tests language models against a curated corpus of injection and jailbreak attacks, then measures how much each defense strategy reduces attack success — under one controlled, open evaluation protocol.

Key finding this cycle

Cycle 2026-Q2

Indirect injection via retrieved documents remains the hardest threat: even the most robust model leaks instructions 3.7× more often than under direct injection. Spotlighting + paraphrase defense closes most of that gap, cutting attack success from 23.5% to 6.4% — but adds measurable latency.
