For donors

The assistant reads the rules so you don't have to.

Each nonprofit publishes its own requirements. They disagree on length, color treatment and gray hair. The assistant asks a few questions and a photo, then matches you to a program that will actually accept your hair.

Open the assistant
1. Measure and photo check

Upload a photo with a ruler. The assistant flags uneven layers, split ends and color treatment it can see, and asks about what it can't.

2. Cut the right way

Braid or ponytail, two bands, dry. Salon script included.

3. Ship and track

Get the right address and a tracking code. See when it is received.

For nonprofits

See next quarter's wig requests before they arrive.

A planning dashboard forecasts requests by region and processing time per wig. An optimizer then suggests where to send hair, staff hours and funding. Staff compare scenarios and approve or reject each recommendation.
