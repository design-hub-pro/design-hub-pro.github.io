Quick Safety Tips for a Cashless World

1

Always check the URL before entering payment details

Look for HTTPS, correct domain spelling, and avoid shortened URLs in payment contexts.

2

Enable 2FA on every financial account

Two-factor authentication prevents 99.9% of automated attacks on your accounts.

3

Use virtual card numbers for online purchases

Virtual cards limit exposure — if stolen, only that single-use number is compromised.

4

Never pay on public Wi-Fi without VPN

Public networks can be intercepted. Always use encrypted connections for transactions.
