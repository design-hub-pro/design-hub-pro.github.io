Abstract / 摘要

This paper presents LaneGuard, an innovative smart device system designed for in-vehicle lane detection and real-time driving feedback. The system integrates advanced computer vision algorithms with edge computing capabilities to provide continuous lane monitoring and instant driver alerts. LaneGuard employs a multi-sensor fusion approach, combining high-resolution camera modules with infrared sensors for all-weather operation, processed through a dedicated neural processing unit (NPU) for low-latency inference. The core innovation lies in our proprietary lane departure prediction algorithm, which anticipates potential deviations 2-3 seconds before occurrence, enabling proactive safety interventions. The companion mobile application provides comprehensive driving analytics, historical trip data, and personalized safety recommendations. Field testing across diverse road conditions demonstrates a 94.7% accuracy rate in lane detection and a 40% reduction in lane departure incidents among users.
