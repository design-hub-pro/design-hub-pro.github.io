Brand Naming & Identity

The moment you name your brand, it gets real. Brainstorm 20, pick 3, lock 1, then design the slogan, personality, and logo concept around it. This is the milestone where your project finally has a face.

⏱ ~75 min
+200 XP
Real-life context What a teen-loved brand identity actually looks like in the wild.
🪪 M2 · Brand

📌 What you'll do

A great brand name is one a teen says out loud and a friend repeats. Aim for repeatable, not clever.

1Brainstorm 10–20 brand names with AI.
2Shortlist your top 3 with reasoning.
3Lock the final brand name + tagline.
4Define brand personality in 5 words.
5Generate a logo concept (sketch or AI image).

💡 AI prompt to start with

Naming
"Generate 15 brand names for a milk tea brand targeting middle and high school students in Irvine. The brand should feel youthful, social, playful, and different from adult-focused tea chains. Mix English and creative invented words. Each name should be 1–2 words and easy to say."
Tip — Say your final name out loud to a real friend. If they smile or repeat it, you've got it. If they ask "wait what?", iterate.
