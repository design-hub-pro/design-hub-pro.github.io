Launch & Showcase

🔒 Locked ⏱ ~75 min +5 馒头 · Final

📌 Instructions Preview · unlock after M6

A pitch isn't a paragraph — it's a story. Hook → problem → product → why it wins. 60 seconds.

1Write a launch title & tagline.
2Write a 1-paragraph product summary.
3Write a "how we're different" paragraph.
4Record a 1-minute showcase pitch.

🎬 Pitch Template (60s)

HOOK (0–10s)
"Hi, I'm [name]. Most teens drink boba 3× a week. None use the brand's app." Pause.

PROBLEM (10–25s)
Why current tea apps fail teens.

PRODUCT (25–45s)
Show your app. Live demo.

WHY IT WINS (45–60s)
"We don't sell tea. We sell the group chat moment."
