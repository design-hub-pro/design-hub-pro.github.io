📌 What you'll do

A pitch isn't a paragraph — it's a story. Hook → problem → product → why it wins. 60 seconds.

1Write a launch title & tagline.
2Write a 1-paragraph product summary.
3Write a "how we're different" paragraph.
4Record a 1-minute showcase pitch.

🎬 Pitch template (60 seconds)

HOOK (0:00 – 0:10)
"Hi, I'm [name]. Most teens drink boba 3× a week. None of them use the brand's app." Pause.

PROBLEM (0:10 – 0:25)
Why current tea apps fail teens — boring rewards, no friends, designed for adults.

PRODUCT (0:25 – 0:45)
Show your app. Live demo. Home → drop → squad order → reward unlock.

WHY IT WINS (0:45 – 1:00)
"We don't sell tea. We sell the group chat moment." End with brand mark.

💡 AI prompt to start with

Pitch
"Write a 60-second pitch script for a teen milk tea app called Boba.Lab. Brand vibe: drop-culture, group-chat-friendly, Gen-Z native. Core features: [paste yours]. Open with a punchy hook, end with a memorable line. Use the structure: hook → problem → product → why it wins. Keep it human, not corporate."
Tip — Practice the pitch 3 times out loud before recording. The first take sounds like a robot. The third sounds like a founder.
