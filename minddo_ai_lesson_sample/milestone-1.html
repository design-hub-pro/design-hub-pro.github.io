Understand the Mission

Get inside the head of your real user. Why a teen-only milk tea brand? What do middle & high schoolers want that adult chains can't give them? This milestone is about finding the angle before you build anything.

⏱ ~45 min
+3 馒头
Real-life context This is your user. A teen, on a school bench, scrolling boba.
★ M1 · Discovery

📌 What you'll do

Start with the user, not the logo. Read the brief, study the teen, find the angle.

1Define your target user in 1 sentence.
2List 3 things teens want that adult tea chains don't offer.
3Pick 3–5 brand keywords for the vibe.
4Write a 2-sentence "brand feeling" statement.

💡 AI prompt to start with

Persona
"Create a detailed persona for a 14-year-old high school student in Irvine, CA who loves boba and TikTok. Include their daily routine, social habits, what makes them buy a drink, and what would make them tell 5 friends about a new tea brand."
Tip — Don't write "teens like fun stuff." Get specific. What app do they open first in the morning? What flavor would they post on a story? Specific = sellable.
