📌 What you'll do

Ship the smallest thing that works, then polish. Don't build it all at once.

1Build a clickable prototype — at least 4 working screens.
2Make the reward + ordering flow work end-to-end.
3Add fake data that feels real.
4Polish copy, spacing, micro-interactions.

📅 3-session game plan

SESSION 01

Skeleton

Generate 4 base screens. Wire navigation. No styling yet.

SESSION 02

Brand Skin

Apply your M5 palette + fonts. Add mock drinks & rewards.

SESSION 03

Polish

Animations, micro-copy, "wow" moments. Test with a friend.

💡 AI prompt to start with

Build
"Generate a single-file HTML+CSS prototype for a teen milk tea app called Boba.Lab. Include 4 screens (Home, Menu, Rewards, Order). Use my color palette: [paste yours]. Add a 'Friday Drop' card on Home, 8 drink cards on Menu, an XP progress ring on Rewards, and a cart with split-pay on Order. Make it tappable between screens with simple JS."
Tip — Show a friend after every session. If they don't get it in 5 seconds, the design is wrong — not them.
