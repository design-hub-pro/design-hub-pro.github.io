Examples from past students

Aisha · 9th grade: "My core 3 are Squad Orders, Friday Drops, and Streak Rewards. Bonus features: Mood Match menu and a 'rate friend's order' poll."

Reward system: XP-based — every drink earns XP, every 100 XP unlocks a sticker, every 5 streak weeks unlocks a secret menu drink.

Social feature: "Sip Squad" — friends see each other's last 3 orders, can react with emojis, and vote weekly on the next group drop.
