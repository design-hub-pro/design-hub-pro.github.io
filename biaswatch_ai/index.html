The Problem

AI Systems Shape Public Discourse—But Who Watches the Watchmen?

Billions of people now rely on AI assistants for information about elections, social movements, and policy debates. Yet these systems can carry subtle—or not so subtle—ideological biases that influence how users perceive reality. Without systematic measurement, we cannot hold AI companies accountable or understand how these biases evolve.

Growth Path

From Research to Movement

Build the Benchmark

Create a curated database of politically significant events and standardized prompts. Test across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and emerging models. Document methodology for academic credibility.

2

Publish & Partner

Release findings through academic papers and open-source tools. Partner with journalism schools, fact-checking organizations, and media literacy nonprofits for validation and reach.

Launch a public dashboard for real-time bias monitoring. Enable community contributions for global coverage. Advocate for AI transparency standards based on collected data.
