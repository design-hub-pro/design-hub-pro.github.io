Dance-Specific Ankle

Relevé Ankle Strengthening

Progressive weight bearing designed for dancers

15 min

Duration

3 sets

Repetitions

Medium

Intensity

Why This Matters for Dancers

Unlike generic PT, this exercise targets the specific ankle stability needed for relevé positions and landing from jumps. It rebuilds the proprioception dancers rely on for balance en demi-pointe.

Exercise Steps

1

Starting Position

Stand facing the barre with feet in parallel, weight evenly distributed

2

Slow Relevé

Rise onto demi-pointe over 4 counts, focusing on rolling through the foot

3

Hold & Balance

Hold at the top for 4 counts. If stable, release one hand from barre

4

Controlled Lower

Lower through the foot over 4 counts. Repeat 8–12 times per set

Note from James Ortiz, DPT

"Stop if pain exceeds 4/10. Soreness is okay — sharp pain is not."
