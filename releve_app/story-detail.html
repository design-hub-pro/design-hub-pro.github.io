Back

I tore my ACL during a grand jeté in rehearsal last March. The sound alone told me everything. In that moment, I wasn't just losing the spring season — I felt like I was losing my identity.

The hardest part wasn't the surgery or the physical rehab. It was watching my company perform without me. It was the voice in my head saying "you'll never be the same."

The biggest shift came when I stopped thinking "I need to fight through this" and started thinking "I need to give myself permission to rest."

Finding a PT who understood dancer biomechanics changed everything. Regular PT told me to "avoid turnout." Dance-specialized PT taught me how to rebuild it safely.

Nine months later, I'm back in the studio. Different? Yes. But honestly — I dance more mindfully now. I listen to my body in a way I never did before. The comeback really is stronger.
