Project Omniverse v0.1
ONLINE CHILD SAFETY · WEB PROTOTYPE

One cruel post in a class group chat. Simulate the 26 classmates around it, then find out what stops it.

Four screens. A pitch page, then a three-step flow: set up the class, watch the group decide, compare the run where nobody intervened against the run where one classmate spoke up.

The crowd, never the child

The targeted student is a node in the graph and nothing more. No model is asked to write as her, and the abusive post itself is never generated.

Silence is a decision too

The feed shows the students who read it and said nothing, with the reason each gave. They are usually the largest group, and most dashboards drop them.

Cut down to what builds

One class, one scenario, three interventions, two runs of five seeds. Roughly $1.40 a comparison. The branching timeline tree and the report generator were cut.

Start at step 1 Jump to the results Designed at 1440 × 900. Every student, run and figure is invented.
