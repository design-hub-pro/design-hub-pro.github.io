Heartbloom

A cheerful guardian travels a candy-colored world, turning sad little creatures back into radiant Blooms — one kind choice at a time.

2D Story RPG Undertale-style Combat Choice-Driven Narrative Lucky Encounters

You are a Bloom Guardian — a keeper of joy in a sunny, candy-colored land. When a creature loses its spark it becomes a Gloom: soft, grey, and droopy. You meet each Gloom in a gentle battle box: dodge its sadness like falling raindrops and bubbles, then choose how to lift its spirits — Cheer, Hug, Sing, or Gift. Every kindness matters, and the road between villages is always full of happy surprises.

Core Systems

Gentle Battle Box

A heart-shaped soul dodges soft raindrop and bubble patterns from each Gloom, while a CHEER / ACT / HUG / GIFT menu runs underneath, Undertale-style — but nothing here can hurt you badly.

Four Ways to Cheer Someone Up

Cheer, Hug, Sing, or Gift. Each Gloom responds to something different — some need a laugh, some need a quiet hug, and figuring out which is half the fun.

Lucky Road Encounters

Traveling between regions triggers happy surprises — traveling merchants, lost baby creatures, festival detours — each with its own short scenario and reward.

Joy / Courage / Wonder

Three tracked traits grow with every kind choice, quietly unlocking new dialogue and deciding which of three warm endings the story blooms into.
