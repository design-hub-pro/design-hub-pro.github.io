First Aid Measures

Eye Contact

Flush with water for 15+ min. Remove contacts. Seek medical attention.

Skin Contact

Remove clothing. Wash with soap and water for 15+ min.

Inhalation

Move to fresh air. Give oxygen if breathing is difficult. Call 911.
