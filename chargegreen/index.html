The Problem

When You Charge Matters More Than You Think

The electricity grid's carbon intensity varies dramatically throughout the day — sometimes by 300% or more. Yet most people charge their devices without knowing whether the electricity is coming from clean solar and wind or dirty coal and gas.

Why This Matters Now

EV Revolution Underway

Battery electric vehicles have 32-47% lower carbon footprints than hybrids. EVs create only 3,932 lbs of CO₂/year vs 11,435 lbs for gasoline vehicles.

Nature Communications

Timing is Everything

CO₂ intensity spikes during evening "peak hours" (6PM-midnight). Charging during daylight hours with solar can reduce emissions by up to 90% compared to night charging.

Environment+Energy Leader

Renewable Grid Growth

A 1% increase in renewable electricity results in a 0.5% decline in carbon footprints. States like California with 30%+ renewables offer significantly cleaner charging.

ScienceDirect

Grid Decarbonization Accelerating

Global CO₂ intensity is expected to fall 3.6% annually, dropping from 445 to 400 g CO₂/kWh by 2027. MIT projects EVs could drop to 50g CO₂/mile with cheap renewables.

Growth Path

From 1 to 100

1

Launch at Your School's EV Charging Stations

Partner with your school's sustainability office to pilot ChargeGreen at campus EV charging stations. Create a "Green Charging Challenge" where students and faculty compete to achieve the lowest carbon charging scores. Track collective impact and share results at school assemblies.

2

Partner with a Local Business or Office Park

Approach local businesses with EV fleets or employee charging stations. Offer to help them track and reduce their charging carbon footprint. Create a monthly "Green Charging Report" showing their environmental impact, which they can use for sustainability reporting and marketing.

3

Present at Clean Energy Conference or Maker Faire

Apply to present ChargeGreen at regional clean energy events, climate tech showcases, or local Maker Faires. Create an interactive demo showing real-time grid carbon intensity and how charging decisions impact emissions. Network with sustainability professionals and potential mentors.

How your environmental passion and volunteer experience leads to climate innovation

Foundation

Service

"Through Girl Scouts and community service, I organized clean-up events and learned how small actions add up. But I kept wondering — how can technology scale these efforts to millions of people?"

Insight

"I discovered that when we charge our devices matters as much as how much we charge. The grid's carbon footprint changes constantly — but nobody knows when it's clean vs. dirty."

Innovation

"ChargeGreen makes invisible data visible. By showing people exactly when their electricity is clean, we can shift millions of charging sessions to low-carbon times — without changing anyone's lifestyle."
