Sleepovers without making it weird

Freshman year I skipped every sleepover because I didn't want to explain my peanut allergy to someone else's parents. That got lonely fast.

Now I just bring my own labeled snack bag and text the host's parent one line ahead of time: "I carry my own food, no big deal, just wanted you to know." Nobody has ever made it a thing. It just needed saying out loud, once.
