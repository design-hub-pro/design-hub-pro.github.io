RideGuard

A bike safety kit that watches speed and traffic, warns of cars behind, and brakes smoothly instead of sharply.

Cycling Safety Radar + Camera Smart Braking Embedded

Parts up close

Three sensors watch the road. One brake acts on what they see.

01

Speed and motion sensor

Knows how fast you go, and whether you are leaning, slowing or falling.

Senses

Wheel speed, lean angle, sudden stops and bumps.

How

A magnet on a spoke passes a small sensor once per turn. A motion chip fills in the gaps about 100 times a second.

Feeds

Every other part. Warnings and braking change with your speed.

Hall sensor + 6-axis IMU ~100 Hz updates Clips to rear hub
02

Rear radar

Sees cars coming up behind you before you hear them.

Senses

Vehicles behind you: how far away and how fast they are closing.

How

A radar chip sends a low-power radio signal backward and times the echo. It works in rain, fog and darkness.

Feeds

A warning on the handlebar screen and a brighter, flashing tail light so the driver notices you.

24 GHz radar Range up to ~140 m (approx.) Tracks several cars at once
03

Smart camera

Catches the risks radar misses.

Senses

Car doors opening, potholes, people stepping off the curb, cars turning across your path.

How

A wide-angle camera feeds a small AI chip on the device. Video is checked on the bike and never uploaded.

Feeds

A warning on the screen. Only a short clip around a near miss is saved, for your own record.

~120° wide-angle lens On-device AI Saves clips only on events
04

Smart brake

Stops you hard enough, never too hard.

Senses

How hard you squeeze the lever, plus speed and risk from the other three parts.

How

A small motor and worm gear sit beside the disc brake. It eases off and re-applies in quick pulses so the wheel never locks.

Acts

Smooths a panic grab so you don't skid or go over the bars. Adds gentle braking if a crash looks close. Your hands always win.

Anti-lock pulsing Caps stopping force near 0.5 g (approx.) Rider override at all times

Numbers marked approx. are design targets based on common parts, not tested results.
