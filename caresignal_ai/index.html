The Problem

The Hidden Crisis of Caregiver Burnout

63 million Americans serve as family caregivers, yet most support systems only intervene after crisis hits. High-risk families often face barriers to seeking help—chronic fatigue, stigma, and limited resources—making early identification nearly impossible.

Why Early Detection Matters

Health Consequences

Caregivers show 25.6% lifetime depression prevalence vs 18.6% in non-caregivers. Chronic stress is linked to hypertension and weakened immune systems.

CDC Research

Support Gap

Only 23% of caregivers report having "good" mental health. Navigating behavioral health systems presents barriers including provider shortages and long wait times.

Caregiver Action Network

Parents at Higher Risk

5-9% of parents experience caregiving burnout, with significantly higher rates among parents of children with special needs due to self-stigma and isolation.

Frontiers in Psychology

Inequitable Access

Over half of caregivers feel they had no choice in becoming caregivers, leading to significantly greater mental health concerns. Many lack resources for formal support.

American Psychiatric Association

Growth Path

From Pilot to Platform

1

Pilot with Partner

Partner with Miracles for Kids to deploy CareSignal AI with 20-30 volunteer families. Collect feedback on check-in burden, insight usefulness, and privacy comfort levels.

2

Validate & Refine

Analyze early warning patterns against actual support interventions. Refine the AI model based on real-world data and collaborate with social workers on insight accuracy.

3

Scale & Share

Open-source the platform for other nonprofits serving vulnerable families. Create integration guides for hospice, pediatric care, and elder care organizations.

Your Journey

From Passion to Purpose

Combining technical excellence with social empathy to create meaningful impact

Foundation

"Through USACO training, I developed rigorous algorithmic thinking—learning to decompose complex problems into solvable components."

Observation

"Volunteering with Miracles for Kids and UNICEF, I saw how families with critically-ill children often go unseen—not because they lack need, but because they lack the bandwidth to ask for help."

Insight

"I realized that traditional support systems react to crises. What if AI could detect stress patterns before families reach breaking points—without adding to their burden?"

Innovation

"CareSignal AI represents my belief that technology should reduce—not increase—barriers. By combining computational methods with human-centered design, we can build tools that serve those who need them most."
