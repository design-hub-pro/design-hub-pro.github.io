Griefwalker

A psychopomp guides restless spirits across the Between — every conversation is a battle, and every battle can end in mercy.

2D Story RPG Undertale-style Combat Choice-Driven Narrative Roguelike Encounters

You are a Griefwalker — a guide for spirits too heartbroken to move on. Their unresolved grief takes physical form as monsters, and you meet each one in a bounded battle box: dodge their pain like bullet-hell projectiles, then choose whether to Fight, Confront, Listen, or Release them. No two spirits resolve the same way twice, and the road between them is never safe from a random encounter.

Core Systems

Bullet-Hell Battle Box

A soul-heart dodges each spirit's grief made manifest as attack patterns — chains, shattered glass, drowning tides — while a FIGHT / ACT / COMFORT / FLEE menu runs underneath, Undertale-style.

Four Ways to End a Fight

Listen, Confront, Release, or Lie. Each spirit responds differently — some need the truth, some need to be let go gently, and lying always has a cost later.

Random Road Events

Travel between realms triggers chance encounters — merchants, lost travelers, rival psychopomps — each with its own short scenario and consequence.

Compassion / Courage / Truth

Three tracked traits shift with every choice, quietly unlocking new dialogue options and changing which of three distinct endings the story resolves toward.
