Chrome Extension · For visually-impaired and dyslexic learners

Accessible Capture

A Chrome extension that turns any web page, PDF, or slide into accessible study material — with smart capture, OCR, read-aloud, and dyslexia-friendly notes.

Originally built around auto-edge-snapping screenshots, now extended into a learning accessibility toolkit that helps low-vision, dyslexic, and ADHD students keep up with mainstream classrooms and online courses.

Built with — not just for — students with disabilities.

For low-vision students
2× upscale, contrast boost, magnifier, and TTS make small print on dense pages legible — without losing context.
For dyslexic students
Lexend font, syllable colors, reading ruler, and one-tap text simplification reduce the cognitive load of reading walls of text.
For blind students
Auto-OCR plus image descriptions sync directly with screen readers like JAWS and NVDA, turning visual content into navigable audio.
For mainstream classrooms
Teachers don't have to maintain separate handouts — students can adapt the same source material to their own needs in one click.
