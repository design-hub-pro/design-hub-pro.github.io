Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is a process used by plants to turn sunlight into food. The plant takes in water and air. It uses light from the sun to make sugar.

Plants have a green colour because of chlorophyll. Chlorophyll lives inside small parts of the cell called chloroplasts. These small parts catch light energy.

Chloroplast (KLOR-uh-plast)
A tiny green factory inside a plant cell. It uses sunlight to make food for the plant.

The plant turns water and the gas carbon dioxide into sugar and the gas oxygen. Animals breathe in this oxygen.

In simple words
