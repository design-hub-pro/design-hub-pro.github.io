Photosynthesis — easy notes

In one line

Plants use sunlight, water, and air to make food and oxygen.

1 What is it?

Photosynthesis is how plants make their own food. Plants take in sunlight, water, and a gas called carbon dioxide. They turn these into sugar (food) and oxygen (a gas we breathe).

Sunlight
Water
CO₂
Sugar (food)
Oxygen

2 Where does it happen?

Inside plant cells, there are tiny green parts called chloroplasts. They hold a green colour called chlorophyll. Chlorophyll catches the sunlight that the plant needs.

3 Why does it matter?

  • Plants make most of the oxygen we breathe.
  • Plants give us food. Animals eat plants.
  • Plants take in CO₂. This helps clean the air.
