What Students Learn
The 5 Pillars of Sound Investing
01

Time in the market > timing the market

Students who ride out the 2008 crash see what selling at the bottom costs. They learn long-term compounding viscerally, not theoretically.

02

Never borrow money to buy stocks

Margin trading is locked behind a lesson. When unlocked, students see exactly how leverage wiped out 1929 and 2008 investors in real time.

03

Control risk before chasing return

Built-in risk score and behavior tracking show students how Sharpe ratio works — and why a 12% steady return beats a chaotic 25% return.

04

Ethics shape long-term returns

ESG metrics are surfaced on every stock. Students who buy tobacco in 1990 see how regulatory and social pressure crushed those stocks long-term.

05

Diversification is the free lunch

The simulator rewards balanced portfolios with the highest risk-adjusted score — teaching the math behind not putting everything in one basket.

Emotional discipline is half the skill

Real news flows during simulations — students see fear, hype, and panic in headlines, and learn to act on numbers, not feelings.
