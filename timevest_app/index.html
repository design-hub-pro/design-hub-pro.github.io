The 6 Pillars of Long-Term Investing
Drawn from The Simple Path to Wealth and a century of market data.
01

Time + compounding are your best friends

The single most reliable wealth formula ever found. Start early, invest steadily, let time do the heavy lifting — and feel the curve bend up.

02

Keep it simple — broad index funds win

Most professionals can't beat the index. Students learn why a boring, diversified fund quietly outperforms the “hot stock” portfolio over 20 years.

03

Never borrow money to buy stocks

Margin is locked behind a lesson. When unlocked, students watch leverage erase 1929 and 2008 investors in real time — and understand why the rule is absolute.

04

Time in the market > timing the market

Students who try to dance in and out see how much they sacrifice. Those who stay invested through every storm see how richly the market rewards patience.

05

Stay the course — don't sell on fear

Real news flows during simulations. The students who hold through 2008 always finish ahead. Emotional discipline becomes muscle memory, not theory.

Live below your means — the gap is your seed

What you don't spend is what you can invest. Students see how a modest monthly contribution, sustained, beats a lucky windfall every single time.
