The research behind the feedback

Pitch is tracked with a YIN-style autocorrelation detector and note onsets with spectral-flux analysis — the same class of methods behind standalone chromatic tuners, run continuously instead of once. The plan logic follows Ericsson's deliberate-practice finding: targeted feedback on a specific weak bar improves faster than repeating a piece end-to-end. Cadenza's role is to find that bar automatically.
