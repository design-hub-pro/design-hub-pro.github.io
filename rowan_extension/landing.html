The three filters

Web content

Fifteen categories, set separately for each child. Block, warn, or allow. Warn is the setting most parents end up using for a teenager, because it loads the page behind a notice and tells you afterwards instead of starting a fight.

See the category list

Music

On YouTube Music, Spotify Web, Apple Music, SoundCloud and Bandcamp, Rowan looks up the track playing and checks its lyrics. It can mute a single line rather than killing the song, which is the difference between a filter a child tolerates and one they route around.

Neither of these is new. Category filters have existed for twenty years and lyric flags for ten. We built them because a family needs one tool rather than three, not because we think they are the hard part.

The hard part is the third one.

Generative AI is not a category you block. It is a thing children now do all day.

Blocking ChatGPT is not an option for a child with homework, and blocking it anyway just moves the activity to a phone. So Rowan does something different. It watches what kind of use is happening and acts on the two kinds that need acting on.

Companion and roleplay bots are off by default under 16

These are the ones designed to be needed rather than used. A child can ask for access with a reason, and you answer from your phone.

Image prompts naming a real classmate are stopped before they send

This is the one risk in the list that points at other people's children. It costs nothing to prevent and a great deal to clean up.

A conversation that reads as a child in trouble gets a helpline, not a block

You are told the category and the time. You are not shown the words. Your child is told, on screen, that this happened.

Four things Rowan cannot do.

Put this next to any competitor's page and see whether they have written one. A filter you trust more than it deserves is worse than no filter, because you stop watching.

It sees one browser on one computer

A phone, a tablet, a games console, a friend's laptop, or Safari on the same machine are all outside it. For most families the phone is the bigger surface, and Rowan does not touch it.

A teenager can uninstall it

You get an email within a minute or so, but the extension cannot stop its own removal. A managed Chrome profile does hold, and setup takes about ten minutes. We walk you through it and we recommend doing it on day one.

Lyric coverage is incomplete and we show you the gap

Lyric databases do not carry user uploads, live sets, mixes, or most small releases. Rowan lists every track it played without being able to check it, so you can see the size of the hole rather than assume there is not one.

Classifying AI use is genuinely hard and we get it wrong

Telling homework help apart from homework substitution is a judgement call that adults disagree about. Rowan gives you a one click correction on every session, and those corrections only train your own household's model.

How a page gets classified, and what leaves the device

Not needed to decide whether Rowan is for you. Here for anyone who wants to check the claim.

Most pages are classified in the browser. A small on device model reads the visible text and the page metadata and returns a category and a confidence score. Nothing leaves the machine for these, which is the large majority of page loads.

When confidence is low, Rowan sends a hash of the address and a short extract to the classifier service and gets a category back. It does not send the page, the images, form fields, or anything typed. The extract is discarded after the call.

Music lookups send the track title and artist as read from the player, and nothing else. AI classification runs entirely in the browser, which is why prompt text is never stored anywhere: it is never sent anywhere either.

What we assume about your household, so you can correct us

Rowan is designed around a shared or semi shared computer, children roughly between 7 and 16, and a parent willing to answer a request within a day. It fits worst where a child has a personal laptop, an admin account, and no expectation of being asked about anything.

We assume you would rather have fewer alerts that you read than many that you skim, so five of the eleven notification types ship switched off. All of them are in Settings if you disagree.

We assume your child will find out how the filter works. Rowan is built so that finding out does not defeat it.
