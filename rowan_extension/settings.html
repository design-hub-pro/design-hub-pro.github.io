Keeping Rowan switched on

A Chrome extension can be turned off by anyone who can reach the extensions page. Here is what actually holds.

Parent PIN on every rule change

Six digits, needed to pause filtering or edit a category. Currently set.

Tell me if the extension is removed or disabled

Email to dana.merrick@gmail.com, usually within a minute. This is a notification, not a lock. Rowan cannot stop a removal.

Force install through a managed Chrome profile

The only setup a determined teenager cannot undo. It takes about ten minutes and a Google Family Link account. If you want the filter to hold rather than to be a signpost, do this one.
