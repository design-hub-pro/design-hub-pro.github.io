The honest version of lyric filtering

No product can promise a clean feed, and any that does is guessing. Lyric databases do not carry every track, and they carry almost nothing for user uploads, live sets, mixes and small releases. Milo played 36 tracks this week that Rowan had no lyrics for. Those played through unchecked.

Rather than hide that, Rowan lists them. If one of them turns out to be a problem, tell us once and every family gets the correction.

One thing this filter does not do

It listens to words, not pictures. A music video on YouTube can be perfectly clean in the lyrics and still show something you would not want a nine year old watching. Video goes through the web content filter instead, which reads the video's own rating and description. If you care most about videos, set video streaming to warn for Milo rather than relying on this page.
