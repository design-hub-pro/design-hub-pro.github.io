What this cannot catch

  • Anything outside Chrome. A phone app, a console, a friend's laptop. Rowan sees one browser on one computer.
  • Images with no text around them. Classification reads text first. A page that is one unlabelled image can slip through until someone reports it.
  • Private or incognito windows, unless you turn the extension on for them in Chrome. Settings shows you how.

We would rather write this here than let you find out from your child.
