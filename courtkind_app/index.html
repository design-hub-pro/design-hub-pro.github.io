CourtKind

Phase 1 · MVP
Observe one court session — with your buddy's profile
The core loop, shipped first. A volunteer opens the app courtside, reads their buddy's profile, follows a predictable session plan, and taps to record what happens in a single session — with in-the-moment tools like the Calm Kit and Star Jar. Everything works offline, in the sun, with kids.

Phase 2 · Insights & Community
Turn logged sessions into insight — shared & private
Once sessions add up, the data does more. Recaps, class rosters, and season progress turn raw logs into insight, then the community layer kicks in: crowdsourced observations — pooled anonymously so no child is identifiable — feed AI pattern recognition and a community strategy Library that spreads what works.
