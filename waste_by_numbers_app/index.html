School cafeterias throw away thousands of pounds of food every year — and nobody's counting. Waste by the Numbers puts an AI food-waste scanner in every student's pocket, turning each tossed tray into data the kitchen can finally act on.

The Problem
Cafeterias waste food blindly —
and it costs schools a fortune.
K-12 cafeterias are one of the largest sources of food waste in the country. Kitchens over-prepare because they're guessing, students toss what they won't eat, and it all disappears into a dumpster — untracked, unmeasured, and unaddressed.

No Data, No Action

Kitchens have no idea which dishes get tossed or how much. Without numbers, nothing changes year after year.

Wasted Budgets

Every tossed serving is real money — funding that could go to fresher food, more options, or smaller class sizes.

A Climate Blind Spot

Rotting food in landfills emits methane. Students rarely connect their lunch tray to the planet — until they see the data.

Our Solution
Four tools that turn a lunch tray
into measurable change.
Built by students, for students. Waste by the Numbers combines an AI scanner, a predictive model, student surveys, and hands-on climate education into one simple app that any school can run.

Snap & Estimate

Point your phone at any tray. AI identifies the food, weighs the waste, and calculates the dollar cost in seconds.

Predict the Trends

A model learns from every scan to forecast which meals will be wasted — so kitchens prep smarter, not more.

Survey the Students

Quick taste & portion surveys feed the kitchen real preferences, replacing guesswork with student voice.

Learn to Compost

Bite-size lessons and quizzes teach composting, recycling, and why saving food protects the planet.

How It Works
From tray to insight in four steps.

1 · Scan

A student snaps their tray at the bin before scraping it.

2 · Measure

AI estimates the weight and cost of what's left over.

3 · Predict

The model spots patterns and forecasts upcoming waste.

4 · Act

Kitchens adjust menus & prep — surveys close the loop.
