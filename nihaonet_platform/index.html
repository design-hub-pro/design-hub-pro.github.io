NihaoNet

An IB Mandarin SL learning hub for students who did not grow up with Chinese

A study site built by an IB Mandarin SL student for her non-native classmates. It does three things in order: explains how characters are actually put together, drills the pinyin and radicals the classroom skips over, then gives listening, reading, writing and speaking practice written against real IB task types. Every answer comes with an explanation of why the wrong options were tempting.

Version one is deliberately small. No streaks, no coins, no AI band scores. Eight desktop screens are prototyped below, including the editor the author uses to change content without going back to a developer.

Design decisions worth arguing about

Three places where the prototype pushes back on the brief.

The brief lists five levels of ten characters. That is fifty characters, and it is not enough on its own.

Fifty is right for version one because each character page has real depth. But the site should say so. A student who finishes all five levels has a method, not a vocabulary. The dashboard copy and level 5 both frame it that way rather than implying the job is done.

Speaking practice cannot be self-marked, so version one should not pretend to mark it.

The brief has students submit a spoken script. Without AI or a teacher there is nobody to respond. The prototype keeps the four-step answer structure and the prompt bank, which are genuinely useful alone, and treats the feedback loop as version two. Better to ship a template that works than a submit button that goes nowhere.

Progress is stored, but nothing gamified was added back in.

This was the easiest place to drift. Every screen here shows counts and completion state and nothing else. No streak, no points, no ranking against classmates. A student who takes two weeks off loses nothing.

Assumptions made in this prototype. Student names, dates, accuracy figures and item counts are sample data for the demo. Chinese texts in the reading and listening sets were written for this prototype and are not from any IB paper. The video card stands in for an embedded YouTube player. The visual system, the module structure and the copy follow the concept document; anything not specified there was decided here and can be changed.
