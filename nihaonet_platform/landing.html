What I kept seeing in class

I am a native speaker sitting in an IB Mandarin SL room full of people who are not. These are the four things that came up again and again, and the site is organised around them.

Characters look random

Students memorise them as pictures. Nobody told them 氵 means water, so 河 湖 海 洗 all had to be learned separately.

Pinyin is a crutch

They can read a sentence out loud from pinyin and still not recognise half the characters when the pinyin is gone.

Speaking runs on a script

Google Translate writes the answer, they read it aloud. Take the paper away and there is no way to build a sentence.

No feedback until next lesson

A practice question with only a right answer teaches nothing. Every item here explains why the other options fail.

This is the part general Chinese sites do not have.

Every answer comes with a reason

A tick mark tells you nothing. On NihaoNet the explanation says which line of the text carries the answer and why each wrong option is tempting. That is the single change my classmates asked for most.

Writing and speaking work the same way. You get a structure, a vocabulary bank for the theme, and a model answer to compare against after you submit your own.

Reading · Theme: Social organization · Q3 of 6 Text type: 通知 notice

为了减少校园垃圾，学生会决定从下个月开始，食堂不再提供一次性餐具。同学们需要自己带餐具，忘记带的同学可以在服务台借用。

学生会做出这个决定的主要原因是什么？
A. 食堂的餐具不够用
B. 学校想要减少垃圾
C. 学生会想省钱
Why B

The first clause opens with 为了 ("in order to"), which marks purpose. Everything after it is the goal: 减少校园垃圾. C is tempting because 一次性餐具 costs money, but cost is never mentioned in the text.

Who it's for

Version one is narrow on purpose. A site that tries to teach everyone Chinese teaches nobody IB Mandarin.

First users
My IB Mandarin classmates

Grade 11 and 12, sitting the paper in under two years. They need gap-filling and past-paper-shaped practice at the same time.

Core users
Any non-native Mandarin SL student

Chinese B SL and HL. Character recognition and handwriting are the weak points, not motivation.

Later, maybe
Beginner learners outside IB

Only once version one is genuinely used. The IB-specific material stays the core either way.

What NihaoNet is not

Cutting these was a decision, not an oversight. Each one either takes attention away from studying or promises something the site cannot honestly deliver yet.

Not a game. No streaks, no coins, no leaderboard. Progress is recorded so you can see what you finished, not so you can compete.
Not an IB grade. AI writing feedback is planned for version two and will flag grammar and phrasing only. It will never produce a band score, and it does not replace your teacher.
Not a past-paper dump. Every text and audio clip is either written by me or an openly licensed source. No copyrighted IB material is uploaded.
Not an app. A website that works on a laptop and a phone browser. A native app is not worth the cost at this size.
