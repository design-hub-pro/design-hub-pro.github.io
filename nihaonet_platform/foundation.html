环
huán
ring; to surround
Level 4 Theme: Sharing the planet
Radical
王jade / precious stone
Structure
Left–right (左右结构)
Words it builds
环境huánjìngenvironment
环保huánbǎoenvironmental protection
循环xúnhuánto cycle, recycling
Example sentence

保护环境是每个人的责任。

Protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility.

Watch out

The left side is 王, not 玉. Students write the extra dot because they know 玉 (jade) first. Also do not confuse 环 with 坏 (huài, bad). One radical apart, opposite feeling.
