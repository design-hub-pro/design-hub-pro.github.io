Text A

关于停止提供一次性餐具的通知

发布单位：明德国际学校学生会 · 9月28日

各位同学：

为了减少校园垃圾，学生会与食堂讨论后决定，从十月一日开始，食堂不再提供一次性筷子、勺子和纸杯。请同学们自己带餐具来学校。

如果当天忘记带，可以在食堂门口的服务台借用一套，用完后洗干净还回来。借用不收费，但一个学期最多借五次。

去年我们统计过，食堂每周大约扔掉四千件一次性餐具。这个数字比我们想的多得多。希望这次改变能让大家慢慢习惯自带餐具。

有问题的同学可以发邮件给学生会。

学生会环保小组

Glossary: 一次性 yīcìxìng · disposable 餐具 cānjù · tableware 借用 jièyòng · to borrow 统计 tǒngjì · to count up
Question 3 Multiple choice · main idea Correct

学生会做出这个决定的主要原因是什么？

A食堂的餐具不够用
B学校想要减少垃圾
C学生会想为学校省钱
D一次性餐具不卫生
Why B, and why the others are tempting

Paragraph 2 opens with 为了减少校园垃圾. 为了 marks purpose, so whatever follows it is the reason. Everything else in that sentence is the consequence, not the cause.

  • C: plausible in real life, but money is never mentioned. Only the borrowing rule says 不收费, and that is about students, not the school's budget.
  • A: the opposite. The canteen is stopping supply by choice, not running short.
  • D: hygiene sounds like an environment topic, but the notice never says it.
Transferable move: in 主旨题, look for 为了 / 因为 / 由于 first. The examiner usually plants the answer right after one of them.
Question 4 True / False with justification Not quite

忘记带餐具的同学需要付钱才能借用。

对 True your answer
错 False correct
一个学期最多借五次
Close, but the wrong line

The line you quoted is about a limit on how many times, not about money. The phrase that settles it is 借用不收费. 收费 means "to charge a fee", and 不 negates it. You read 最多借五次 as a restriction and assumed a cost came with it. In True/False questions the justification has to contain the actual claim, not something near it.
