Methodology

A forced-choice experiment in type-UI fit

Participants see one mockup at a time, rendered in four randomized typographic versions (A, B, C, D — order shuffled). They pick the version that feels right and rate their confidence. We collect a forced choice per mockup plus a 5-point confidence slider, then estimate pairwise preferences with a Bradley-Terry model.
