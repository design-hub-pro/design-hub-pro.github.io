Growth Path

From Pilot to Platform

1

Family Pilot

Start by recording stories from your own family. Test the app with grandparents or elderly relatives, capturing 5-10 stories. Refine the AI prompts based on what questions work best.

2

Community Expansion

Partner with local senior centers, cultural organizations, or immigrant community groups. Train volunteers to conduct oral history sessions. Build a collection of 50+ interconnected stories.

3

Archive Partnership

Connect with university digital humanities programs or public libraries. Contribute stories to established oral history archives while maintaining AI-powered discovery features for your platform.
