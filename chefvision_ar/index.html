Growth Path

From Prototype to Product

1

Build MVP with Smartphone AR

Start with a smartphone-based AR cooking assistant using ARKit/ARCore. Partner with local cooking schools to test with students and gather feedback on recipe guidance.

2

Integrate Smart Glasses

Expand to lightweight AR glasses for true hands-free cooking. Partner with appliance manufacturers to add IoT integration for automated temperature and timer control.

3

Scale to Smart Kitchens

Launch B2B solutions for culinary schools and restaurants. Create partnerships with grocery chains for ingredient-to-recipe integration and meal planning services.
