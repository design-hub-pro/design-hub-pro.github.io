Limitations

  • SGP4 propagation degrades beyond 7-day windows; LSTM jitter feature partly compensates.
  • Side-payments assume fungible inter-agency budgets — geopolitically optimistic.
  • Capture mechanics modelled as instantaneous; real grappling adds 30–90 min per target.
  • Drag model excludes solar-cycle extremes (F10.7 > 220).
