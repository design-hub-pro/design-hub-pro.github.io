Research Question 03

Can a game-theoretic mechanism beat unilateral cleanup?

Each agency pays to remove debris and benefits from a safer orbital commons. Removing debris that threatens others is a positive externality — selfish play under-supplies cleanup. We model debris removal as a repeated public-goods game with side-payments and ask whether a VCG-style auction reaches the social optimum.
