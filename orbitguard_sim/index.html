Three research questions

Prediction · Decision · Coordination

RQ1 · Prediction

Can ML predict high-risk collision zones?

XGBoost + LSTM ensemble trained on 1.4M Space-Track CDMs reaches AUC 0.943 with a 38.7-hour median lead time — almost double SGP4-only baselines.

RQ2 · Decision

How should agents pick targets under limited fuel?

Sixteen autonomous cleanup satellites — Sweepers, Falcons, Carriers, Scouts — each with bounded Δv. They bid on debris lots ranked by cascade-weighted Pc.

RQ3 · Coordination

Can game theory beat selfish play?

A VCG-style auction with Pigouvian launch tax flips Defect–Defect into the unique Nash equilibrium — 94% cooperation vs. 21% in the status-quo game.
