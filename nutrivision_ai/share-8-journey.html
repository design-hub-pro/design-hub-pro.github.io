HOW IT WORKS

From Photo to Health Intelligence

STEP 1 Scan Your Meal

Point your camera at any meal. Our computer vision model identifies every ingredient, estimates portions using 3D depth sensing, and calculates precise macros in under 0.3 seconds.

STEP 2 Twin Processes

Your metabolic digital twin — trained on your biometrics, CGM data, and meal history — simulates exactly how your body will respond: glucose spikes, energy levels, nutrient absorption.

STEP 3 Predict & Optimize

AI generates predictive insights: what nutrients you're missing, how your meal timing affects sleep quality, and what to eat next to hit your goals. Nutrition becomes proactive, not reactive.

STEP 4 Coach Adapts

Your AI coach learns from every interaction and evolving health data. It continuously refines your meal plans, adjusts goals, and delivers personalized guidance that gets smarter every day.
