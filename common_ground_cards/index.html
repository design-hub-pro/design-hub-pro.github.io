Common Ground
Web app · 7 screens

Your commons are
somebody's first deck.

Every booster pack leaves a pile of cards nobody wants. Common Ground collects that pile, sorts it into balanced 40-card decks, and puts them in the hands of kids at schools, hospitals and deaf education programs. Then a volunteer sits down and plays a round.

40

One deck recipe, no exceptions

Commons and uncommons only, HP capped at 110, no three-energy attacks. Two kids at a table have the same odds, so the game is decided by how they play it.

2.5h

Sorting is the real cost

A shoebox of 1,500 cards takes about 2.5 volunteer hours and $18 in materials. The cards themselves are worth nothing, which is the whole point.

0

Sessions that run without access

A session at a deaf or hard-of-hearing site does not happen without a Deaf volunteer or a booked interpreter. The date moves instead.

About this prototype

Seven working screens built at 1440 by 900. All names, sites, numbers and notes are sample data written to be plausible, not records of a real organisation. Card art shown in the sort station is drawn in CSS and is not reproduced from any real card.

Common Ground is an independent concept. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by The Pokémon Company International, Nintendo, Creatures Inc. or GAME FREAK. A real version of this project would need a written policy on donated card handling and, before claiming tax-deductible status, a 501(c)(3) determination letter.
