Access notes on file
Every session needs a Deaf volunteer or a booked ASL interpreter. No exceptions, and we book before the box ships.
Rules sheet is the visual version: symbols and arrows, English kept under 60 words.
Anime episodes need captions on and a sub track. Terrence picks the episode a week ahead.
Turn order is signalled with the laminated card, not by calling out. Table gets a lamp, not a buzzer.
