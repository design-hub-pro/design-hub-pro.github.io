Project in Action

Real-world scenarios showing how BusBeacon AI keeps students safe

Automatic Boarding Detection

When Emma steps onto Bus #247, her Bluetooth beacon tag automatically registers with the bus sensor. Parents receive instant notification.

Real-Time Parent Updates

Parents track the bus location in real-time and receive ETA updates, so they know exactly when to meet their child at the stop.

School Fleet Management

Administrators monitor all buses in real-time, track attendance automatically, and respond quickly to any route delays or emergencies.

Safe Drop-off Confirmation

Parents receive confirmation the moment their child exits at the correct stop. Alert triggers if child exits at an unexpected location.

System Architecture

Growth Path

From Pilot to Platform

1

Pilot with One School

Partner with a local elementary school to test the system with 2-3 buses. Collect feedback from parents, drivers, and administrators to refine the app and beacon hardware.

2

Expand to District

Scale to the full school district with fleet-wide deployment. Integrate with existing school management systems for seamless attendance tracking and parent communication.

3

Regional Platform

Offer BusBeacon AI as a SaaS platform for multiple school districts. Add advanced features like AI-powered route optimization, predictive ETAs, and multi-language support.
