Inside Outside Touch

Ball Mastery
Intermediate
2 min

Description

Start with the ball at your feet. Using alternating touches, push the ball with the outside of your foot, then bring it back with the inside.

Why This Matters 🧠

🧠
Game Intelligence

Inside-outside touches help you change direction under pressure. In a match, this lets you beat a defender 1v1 by shifting the ball quickly from one foot path to another — creating space to pass or shoot.

Game Scenario 🏟️

"Imagine you're on the wing with a defender closing in. How would you use this touch to create space for a cross?"
