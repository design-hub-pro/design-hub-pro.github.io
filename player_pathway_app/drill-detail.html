Inside Outside Touch

Ball Mastery
Intermediate
2 min

Description

Start with the ball at your feet. Using alternating touches, push the ball with the outside of your foot, then bring it back with the inside.

Coaching Points

Keep your head up and scan your surroundings
Stay on the balls of your feet for quick reactions
Increase speed gradually as you build confidence

Common Mistakes

Touching the ball too far away from your body
Flat-footed stance reducing agility and balance
