Player Pathway

Structured soccer skill training through short-form videos, guided plans, and gamified progression for youth players aged 7-12.

User Flow
How Players Use the App
1 Home DashboardPlayer opens the app, sees streak, today's session, and weekly progress at a glance
2 TrainPicks a quick session or follows the weekly plan with guided drill sequences
3 LibraryBrowses the full video catalog, filters by skill category, continues in-progress lessons
4 Drill DetailTaps a drill to watch the demo video, read coaching tips, and start the timer
5 Active SessionFollows along with the live countdown, completes each drill, and marks it done
6 ProgressReviews XP earned, skill improvements, weekly activity chart, and achievement badges
7 ProfileManages player info, views collected badges, adjusts notifications and settings
