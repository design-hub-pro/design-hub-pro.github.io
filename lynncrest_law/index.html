The EB-1A Green Card

Do I qualify for the EB-1A?

The EB-1A is reserved for individuals of extraordinary ability. You may be a strong candidate if the following describe you:

◆I can demonstrate extraordinary ability in science, art, education, business, or athletics through sustained national or international acclaim.
◆My achievements are recognized in my field and are extensively documented.
◆I am coming to the United States to work in the area of my extraordinary ability for the purpose of benefiting the USA.
◆I do not necessarily need to have an offer of employment.

The USCIS Standard

Do I meet the 10 Criteria of the EB-1A?

Petitioners typically satisfy at least three of the following categories of evidence:

  1. Documentation of a nationally or internationally recognized prize or award for excellence in my field.
  2. Evidence of authorship of scholarly articles in professional or major trade publications or other major media.
  3. Evidence of the display of my work at artistic exhibitions or showcases.
  4. Published material about me in professional or major trade publications or other major media — including title, date, author, and any necessary translation.
  5. Evidence that I performed in a leading or critical role for organizations with a distinguished reputation.
  6. Evidence of participation, individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in my field or an allied specialization.
  7. Evidence that I commanded a high salary or other significantly high remuneration relative to others in the field.
  8. Evidence of original scientific, scholarly, artistic, athletic, or business-related contributions of major significance.
  9. Evidence of commercial success in the performing arts, shown by box office or sales figures.
  10. Where standards do not readily apply to my occupation, comparable evidence may be submitted to establish eligibility.
