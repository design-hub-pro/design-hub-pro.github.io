How a track gets its score

Four signals, measured while a track plays. The score is the average focused minutes that track produced for you, scaled 0 to 100.

Typing rhythm

weight 40%

We record the gaps between keystrokes, never the keys. A steady 150 to 400 ms rhythm means you are writing. Long bursts then silence usually means you are stuck or drifting.

Tab switching

30%

Switching to Classroom or a PDF is fine. Switching to YouTube or Instagram counts against the track.

Idle time

20%

No input for 90 seconds is idle. Reading a long page is allowed when you are scrolling.

Webcam gaze

10%, optional

Off unless you turn it on. Runs on-device. Only "looking at screen" yes or no is kept.

Your focus data is yours.

Everything is stored in your browser. There is no FocusScore account and no server that sees your sessions. If you want to share a report with a parent or tutor, you export it yourself.
