Methodology and metric definitions

Cooperation index — share of match time the alliance spends targeting non-overlapping field zones, 0–1.

Adaptability score — win-rate retention when the opponent roster is swapped mid-batch without retraining, 0–1.

Decision latency — wall-clock time from sensor read to committed action, averaged per cycle.

All figures come from simulated matches on FTC-Sim's physics-approximate engine, not from physical robots. Treat as directional, not as a substitute for on-field testing.
