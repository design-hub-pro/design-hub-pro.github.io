Why teams use FTC-Sim

Every strategy question your team argues about, answered with data.

Scale past the practice field

Run 10,000 matches against a variant opponent in the time it takes to drive one real match. No field time, no broken servos.

Compare agent architectures

Put a hand-tuned state machine against a PPO policy against an LLM tactical agent, under identical field conditions.

Reproducible, exportable

Fixed seeds, versioned game configs, and a full decision log per robot — every run can be replayed or cited.

How it works

From roster to results in four steps.

  1. 1

    Configure the field

    Pick the season's game, field variant, and scoring rules.

  2. 2

    Assign four agents

    Rule-based, RL, or LLM — mixed freely across both alliances.

  3. 3

    Simulate in batch

    Run hundreds to millions of matches with sensor-noise and randomized starts.

  4. 4

    Read the reasoning

    Open any match and see exactly why each agent made its call.
