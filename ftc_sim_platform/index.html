FTC-Sim prototype v0.1

A multi-agent simulator for FTC alliance strategy — seven screens, from pitch to per-robot reasoning log.

FTC-Sim runs thousands of simulated matches between rule-based, reinforcement-learning, and LLM-based robot agents, so a team can compare alliance strategies without building or driving a physical robot. This prototype covers the marketing page, sign-in, the run dashboard, the simulation builder, a single match with per-agent reasoning, the cross-strategy comparison lab, and settings.

Scale past the field

10,000 matches in the time one real match takes to drive.

Three agent architectures

Rule-based, RL, and LLM agents mixed freely on either alliance.

Per-robot reasoning

Every decision — chain-of-thought, Q-values, or state transition — is logged and replayable.
