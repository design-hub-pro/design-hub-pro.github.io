The Problem

Invisible Chemical Hazards in Performance Spaces

Stage and rehearsal spaces are high-density, semi-enclosed environments where frequent use of cleaning agents, sprays, adhesives, and theatrical materials leads to accumulation of volatile organic compounds (VOCs), particulate matter (PM2.5), and CO₂. These chemical exposures are invisible and often lack real-time warning systems, leaving backstage workers and students facing overlooked health risks.

Social Impact

Protecting Performers and Crew Health

Respiratory Health in Theater

A University of British Columbia study found theatrical workers exposed to stage fog and effects had lower lung function and higher rates of chronic respiratory symptoms including wheezing, chest tightness, and shortness of breath.

PubMed Research

Cognitive Performance Impact

Harvard research demonstrated that VOC exposure significantly impairs cognitive function. Task accuracy decreased by 4.9% when exposed to high TVOC levels, with impacts on attention, memory, and decision-making.

Wiley Research

Chemical Hazards Backstage

Scene shops use solvents, adhesives, spray paints, and plastic resins that produce toxic fumes. Theatrical fog fluids can decompose into formaldehyde and acrolein when overheated.

CCOHS Guidelines

Mental Health Connection

Harvard's Healthy Buildings program found that poor indoor air quality directly impacts mental health. Elevated CO₂ and PM2.5 are linked to increased anxiety, reduced focus, and impaired well-being.

Harvard Healthy Buildings

From Pilot to Platform

1

School Theater Pilot

Deploy sensors in your school's main stage, scene shop, and rehearsal spaces. Collect baseline air quality data during productions and integrate with existing stage lighting systems to demonstrate the concept.

2

Research Partnership

Partner with university environmental health departments to validate findings and publish research. Connect with occupational health organizations like CCOHS to establish industry-relevant safety thresholds.

3

Industry Expansion

Scale to community theaters, dance studios, and professional venues. Develop standardized installation kits and partner with theater equipment suppliers to integrate StageSafe into new venue designs.
