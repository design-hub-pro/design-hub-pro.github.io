Maillard Reaction

Amino acid + Reducing sugar → Browning

140-165°C AGEs concern

When amino acids react with reducing sugars at heat, hundreds of flavor compounds form. For diabetics, excess AGEs (Advanced Glycation End-products) accelerate vascular damage.

Starch Gelatinization

Granule swelling → Gel formation

60-80°C Texture control

Starch granules absorb water and swell irreversibly. For seniors with dental issues, controlled gelatinization creates soft, moist textures that are easy to chew.
