Phase 3 — Communication mismatch analysis

n = 14 coded interviews, 27 survey responses — exploratory, not powered for significance

Youth and elders diverge sharply on silence, patience, and structure — but land close together on feeling understood.

That gap is the project's central evidence: a mismatch in style doesn't automatically produce a mismatch in connection. Read as directional, given small group sizes (n=8 youth interviews, n=6 elder interviews).
