Survey instrument v1

Drafted from Phase 1 interview themes. 1–5 agreement scale.

  1. Even without constant talking, I can feel connected to someone.
  2. I'm willing to wait when someone needs time to find their words.
  3. Even when we disagree on something important, I can still feel understood.
  4. I prefer a specific question or topic over fully open conversation.
  5. I don't need frequent or very close contact to feel understood.
  6. Doing an activity together can build connection more easily than talking.
  7. A quiet, low-stimulation setting makes it easier for me to communicate.
  8. Music, photos, or familiar objects sometimes help me express myself better than words.
  9. I keep talking more easily when others don't rush to correct or finish my sentences.
  10. After my most recent intergenerational talk, I felt understood in how I wanted to be communicated with.
