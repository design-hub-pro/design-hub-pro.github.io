Phase 4–5 — Low-pressure communication modes

Four low-pressure formats, designed so no one is required to connect through open-ended chat. Each pairing runs once, then answers a 6-item Mini Feedback.

Conversation Cards

Both pick from a deck of specific prompts. Any card can be skipped.

Observes: whether structure lowers pressure
3 sessions piloted

Music Exchange

Each shares a song or sound. Explaining it is optional — listening together is enough.

Observes: whether non-verbal media help expression
4 sessions piloted

Object / Photo Prompt

One brings an object or photo as an anchor for the conversation.

Observes: whether concrete objects beat abstract chat
2 sessions piloted

Quiet Co-activity

Walking, drawing, tidying, or listening together — no requirement to keep talking.

Observes: whether shared silence still connects
Scheduled Week 9
