Allulose

C₆H₁₂O₆

Diabetes-targeted sweetener substitution

Chemical mechanism

Allulose is a C-3 epimer of fructose. Its free carbonyl group still enters the Maillard reaction with amino acids, so baked goods brown and develop flavor like sugar-sweetened versions. But it is barely metabolized — ~70% is absorbed then excreted unchanged in urine, contributing ≈0.4 kcal/g and provoking almost no insulin response.

Unlike erythritol, allulose does brown — critical for banana bread, cookies, and crème brûlée.
