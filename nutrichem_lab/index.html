NutriChem Lab

A Food-Chemistry Substitution Database
for Healthier Senior Cooking

A food-science research project: build a chemically-grounded ingredient-substitution database, validate it through real kitchen experiments and data analysis, and deliver it to older adults as a simple app. Chemistry first — data science and AI are the tools, not the point.
