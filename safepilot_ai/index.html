Growth Path

From Prototype to Industry Standard

1

Campus Golf Cart Pilot

Partner with a university or retirement community to test on golf carts. Start with basic pedestrian detection and speed limiting in controlled environments.

2

Warehouse Forklift Integration

Expand to warehouse environments with forklifts. Add trajectory prediction and CAN bus integration for automatic speed control. Target OSHA compliance benefits.

3

Fleet-Wide Deployment

Launch enterprise solution with fleet management dashboard, compliance reporting, and insurance partnerships. Target large logistics and manufacturing companies.
