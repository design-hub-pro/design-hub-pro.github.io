Catch a Planet Crossing Its Star

When a planet passes in front of its star, the star dims by about 1% for a few hours. Book a slot on a global robotic telescope, image the star through the transit, and build the light curve yourself. Refined timings from students help keep the orbits of known planets up to date — and occasionally catch the first sign of a brand-new world.
