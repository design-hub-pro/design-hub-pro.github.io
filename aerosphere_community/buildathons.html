How a Build-a-thon Works

1. Form a team

Join solo and get matched, or bring 2–5 friends. Beginners welcome.

2. Get your kit

Parts, datasets, and a starter guide ship free to every registered team.

3. Build with mentors

Engineers from partner orgs drop into your channel all weekend.

4. Hand it over

Winning builds get deployed with the partner community, for real.

Build it in a weekend. Watch it help someone for years.
