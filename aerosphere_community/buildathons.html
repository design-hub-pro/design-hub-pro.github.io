How an Observe-a-thon Works

1. Form a team

Join solo and get matched, or bring 2–5 friends. Beginners welcome.

2. Get scope time

Telescope slots, target lists, and data pipelines are booked for every team — free.

3. Reduce with mentors

Astronomers from partner observatories drop into your channel all weekend.

4. Submit the result

Solid measurements go into real archives like AAVSO and the MPC, with your name.

One weekend of photons. A data point that outlives the night.
