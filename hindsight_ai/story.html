What I wish I knew before junior year

Jordan T.

Junior → Senior year, public high school

"I overloaded on APs to look impressive, but burned out by October. Depth beat breadth for me."

Junior year I signed up for five AP classes because that's what everyone said colleges wanted. By week six I was pulling all-nighters and my grades in the classes I actually cared about started slipping.

What actually moved the needle: two APs done well, one real extracurricular I stuck with, and sleep. Admissions officers told a friend's parent they'd rather see depth in fewer things.
