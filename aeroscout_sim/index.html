Pure simulation, no hardware

Models flight kinematics, detection geometry and battery depletion in software. No physical drones required to study the design space.

Four coordination strategies

Static grid, sector relay, risk-weighted and adaptive swarm. Compare detection time, coverage and energy on the same scenario.

Cost-aware fleet planner

Given a target SLA and budget, the planner sweeps 5,000+ configurations and recommends the Pareto-optimal mix.
