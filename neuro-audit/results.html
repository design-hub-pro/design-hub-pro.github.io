Claims Detected

"Hating loud noises is a sign of ADHD"
FALSE
DSM-5 Truth:

Sound sensitivity is NOT a diagnostic criterion for ADHD. This may indicate sensory processing differences or other conditions like autism spectrum disorder.

"If you zone out during conversations, you definitely have ADHD"
MISLEADING
DSM-5 Truth:

While inattention IS a criterion, diagnosis requires 6+ persistent symptoms across multiple settings for 6+ months, plus functional impairment. One symptom ≠ diagnosis.

"Everyone with ADHD is hyperactive"
OVERSIMPLIFIED
DSM-5 Truth:

ADHD has 3 subtypes: Predominantly Inattentive, Predominantly Hyperactive-Impulsive, and Combined. Not everyone shows hyperactivity.

Actual DSM-5 Criteria

ADHD requires:

  • 6+ symptoms from inattention OR hyperactivity-impulsivity criteria
  • Symptoms present for 6+ months
  • Symptoms present before age 12
  • Symptoms in 2+ settings (home, school, work)
  • Clear evidence of functional impairment
  • Symptoms not better explained by another disorder

If You're Concerned

Talk to a licensed professional: Only qualified clinicians can diagnose ADHD using standardized assessments.

Learn from reliable sources: CHADD.org, ADDitude Magazine, CDC ADHD guidelines.

Avoid self-diagnosis: TikTok symptoms checklists are not medical tools.
