The Crisis We're Solving

The News Hook (2024-2025)

  • "TikTok made me think I have ADHD" – Gen Z self-diagnosis epidemic
  • 84% of mental health advice on TikTok is misleading (2024 studies)
  • Teens diagnosing based on vague symptoms instead of clinical criteria
  • Increased inappropriate medication requests from self-diagnosers

Neuro-Audit Solution

  • AI transcription (OpenAI Whisper) of viral videos
  • Semantic matching against official DSM-5 diagnostic criteria
  • Classification system: Medically Accurate, Vague, or Misinformation
  • Twitter bot auto-replies to viral misinformation with facts

Core Technology

OpenAI Whisper

State-of-the-art speech recognition to transcribe video audio with 95%+ accuracy across accents and audio quality

Semantic Matching

Vector embeddings compare influencer claims against DSM-5 medical text to identify contradictions and inaccuracies

DSM-5 Database

Official diagnostic criteria for ADHD, autism, and other mental health conditions used as ground truth

AI Classification Pipeline

  • Video download and audio extraction
  • Whisper transcription with timestamps
  • NLP claim extraction from transcript
  • Vector similarity scoring vs DSM-5
  • Multi-class classification output

Twitter Fact-Check Bot

  • Auto-detects viral mental health tweets
  • Replies with DSM-5 verified facts
  • Cites credibility score and sources
  • Links to professional resources (CHADD, CDC)
  • Rate-limited to avoid spam detection

Science Fair Research

Publishable hypothesis testing

Hypothesis:

"The correlation between 'Viral Potential' (view count) and 'Medical Accuracy' – do wrong videos go viral faster?"

Finding:

r = -0.73, p < 0.001
Misinformation videos are 24x more viral than accurate content
