Attune

A cognitive operating system for attention. It measures how long your attention holds in six different ways, trains it, then checks how much of that survives at a real desk with a real phone on it.
Attention TrainingDistraction TrackingAdaptive LearningTransfer
The argument is on screen 11. Trained attention that does not survive a phone lying on the desk is not attention, it is a lab result. Almost nobody measures the gap, so almost nobody knows whether their training worked.
