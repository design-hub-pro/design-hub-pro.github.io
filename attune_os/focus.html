The attention economy · 4 of 9
Every medium that competes for attention eventually learns to measure it. What gets measured gets optimised, and what gets optimised is rarely the thing the reader came for.
The important distinction is between attention that is captured and attention that is given. Captured attention is involuntary. It answers a stimulus. Given attention is a decision that has to be renewed, second by second, against everything else the mind could be doing instead.
That renewal is the skill. It is also the part almost no product is designed to strengthen.
