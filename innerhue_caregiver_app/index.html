Why the parent, not the teen

In Family-Based Therapy, the parent is the active agent of recovery.

Phase 1 of FBT asks parents to take full control of plating, serving, and supervising every meal. The teen is too unwell to drive recovery — but the parent is doing the hardest, most consequential job in adolescent mental health, often without support. Innerhue is built for them.
