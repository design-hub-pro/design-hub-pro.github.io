🗺️ Suggested Website Layout

The level map for your build.

Not sure where to start? Follow this stage-by-stage blueprint for your page.

1

🦸 Hero Section

Introduce Build Day with a powerful headline and a button like “Bring Build Day to Your School.”

2

❓ Why Build Day?

Explain the problem: many students play games, but far fewer ever learn to create them.

3

🎮 What Students Will Do

Describe activities: designing a world, learning basic coding, building obstacles, and sharing the final project.

4

🗓️ Event Schedule

Show a simple, realistic day plan — opening to awards.

9:00 AMWelcome & Creator Kickoff
10:00 AMGame Design Mini-Lesson
11:00 AMBuild Your First World
12:00 PMLunch & Idea Sharing
1:00 PMCoding & Game Mechanics
2:30 PMTeam Build Challenge
3:30 PMStudent Showcase
4:00 PMAwards & Next Steps
5

🏫 Benefits for Schools

Explain how the event supports STEM learning, creativity, collaboration, digital literacy & entrepreneurship.

6

📣 Call to Action

End with a clear invitation: “Host Build Day at Your School.”
