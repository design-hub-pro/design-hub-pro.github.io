The Problem

The Hidden Cost of Undetected Equine Injuries

Lameness is the single most costly health condition in the equine industry, yet most cases go undetected until visible symptoms appear. Riders and trainers rely on subjective visual assessment, which misses subtle gait changes that precede serious injuries. By the time a horse visibly limps, the underlying condition has often progressed significantly, leading to longer recovery times, higher veterinary costs, and preventable suffering.

Growth Path

From Stable to Stadium

1

Pilot with Local Equestrian Centers

Partner with 3-5 local riding schools and training barns to test the sensor prototype on diverse horse breeds and disciplines. Collect baseline gait data and validate the AI detection model against professional veterinary assessments.

2

Expand to Competitive Circuits

Bring StrideSync AI to regional dressage, show jumping, and eventing competitions. Offer trainers a data-driven edge for optimizing training programs and ensuring horses peak at the right time.

3

Build an Open Equine Health Platform

Create an open data repository of anonymized gait patterns that veterinary researchers and equine scientists can access. Partner with vet schools to advance lameness research and develop breed-specific detection models.
