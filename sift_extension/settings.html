Which engine scores your comments

The only setting in Sift with a real privacy cost attached. Read both.

We recommend the local engine, even though it is the less accurate one. A tool whose pitch is "you control what reaches you" should not require handing a third party every comment you read.

What is stored, and where

DataLocationSize
Model weightsIndexedDB24.8 MB
Your 147 correctionsIndexedDB62 KB
Thresholds and site ruleschrome.storage.sync4 KB

No comment text is stored, except inside the 147 corrections. Erasing the set deletes those sentences too.

Sharing

Anonymous counts

Number scored and number hidden, nothing else. No text, no site, no author.

Contribute corrections to research

Off, and staying off in this version. A shared pool is the obvious next feature and the one most likely to leak something personal.

Crash reports

Stack traces only, sent when the extension throws.

Permissions Sift asked for

storageYour settings and correction set.
scriptingInjecting the content script that finds comments.
hostFour domains only: reddit.com, x.com, instagram.com, youtube.com.

Not requested: browsing history, tabs, bookmarks, downloads, or all-sites access.
