People you talk to

The most common false positive is warmth between friends. "Shut up, you're the worst" from someone you reply to daily is not an attack, and no model reading one sentence can tell. Sift counts your exchanges with an account and raises its threshold.

The obvious failure. A person you talk to a lot can also be the person hurting you. The lift is capped at +0.15, threats ignore it completely, and one tap on "not a friend" removes an account for good.

What a hidden comment looks like

There is no option to delete a comment from the page. Knowing something was hidden, and being able to look, is what keeps this a volume knob rather than a filter bubble.
