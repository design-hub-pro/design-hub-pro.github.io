Why NutriScope AI?

The Problem

  • Gen Z least likely to read nutrition labels (2024 health survey)
  • Ultra-processed foods now make up 70% of teen diets
  • Hidden sugars & additives require chemistry knowledge to decode
  • Long-term health impacts invisible until it's too late

Our Solution

  • Instant AI scanning – no manual label reading required
  • Plain language translation of complex chemical ingredients
  • Predictive health modeling shows long-term risks (cardiovascular, diabetes, weight gain)
  • TikTok integration turns scans into shareable educational content

Core Features

Computer Vision AI

YOLOv8 or Google Vision API detects food labels with 95%+ accuracy, extracting nutrition facts and ingredient lists instantly

NLP Translation

Large Language Models simplify chemical ingredients (E-numbers, additives) into plain English with health impact explanations

Predictive Modeling

AI models estimate short & long-term metabolic impacts based on glycemic load, sodium thresholds, and additive profiles

Auto TikTok Video Generation

  • AI generates 15-60 second educational videos from scan results
  • Customizable styles: shocking facts, informative, humorous
  • AI-generated piano background music (student's creative touch)
  • One-tap sharing to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

Chemistry Decoder

  • Decodes E-numbers (E421 = high fructose corn syrup)
  • Highlights preservatives, artificial flavors, colorings
  • Explains health impacts in simple terms
  • Links to peer-reviewed research studies
