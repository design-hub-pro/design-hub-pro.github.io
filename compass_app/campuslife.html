How things work at Lincoln High
Dress Code
Collared shirt required Mon–Thu. No hats indoors. Fridays are spirit days.
Block Schedule
4 classes a day, 85 minutes each. Classes rotate A/B day.
Cafeteria
Grab a tray, scan your ID, sit anywhere — tables aren't assigned.
Assemblies
Sit with your grade. Announced the morning of over the intercom.
Good to know
Raise your hand for the hall pass — no asking out loud needed.
Locker combos come in your welcome email — 3 turns, right-left-right.
Spirit Fridays = wear school colors, navy and gold.
Office hours: any teacher, any time, Room 210 — no appointment needed.
