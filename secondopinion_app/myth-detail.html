Myth #218
MISLEADING

"An AI chatbot said my mole probably isn't cancer, so I can skip the biopsy."

Common AI chatbot response · stated with 89% confidence

Why this is misleading

No visible reasoning

The model doesn't show which features of the photo drove its answer — a "black box" a doctor can't audit or double-check.

Trained on limited skin tones

Public dermatology image sets skew toward lighter skin, so accuracy drops for patients it saw less of during training.

The answer shifts with framing

Describing the same photo as "a spot" versus "a growing mole" can change the verdict — wording shouldn't change a diagnosis.

Verified answer

Dr. Priya Anand
Dermatology · Verified MD · Reviewed in 14 min

A photo check can't feel texture, measure depth, or track how a mole changes over months. It also performs worse on darker skin, where melanoma is often caught later. If a mole is new, changing, or irregular, a biopsy is still the only way to confirm — think of the AI's read as a prompt to get checked sooner, never a reason to skip the exam.
