Generic playlists miss the emotional context that makes music truly therapeutic.

Music as Medicine for the Soul

Cognitive Preservation

Music therapy reduces agitation and improves cognitive function in dementia patients. Personalized music tied to autobiographical memories activates brain regions that generic music cannot reach.

Social isolation increases mortality risk by 26%. Conversational AI combined with music provides meaningful engagement, while volunteer band visits create in-person social connections for isolated seniors.

Growth Path

From Pilot to Platform

1

Senior Center Pilot

Partner with a local senior center or assisted living facility. Test the chatbot with 10-15 residents, collecting memories and refining the NLP engine based on real conversations and musical preferences.

2

Volunteer Band Network

Recruit local music students and community musicians to form volunteer bands. Build a scheduling system connecting bands with senior facilities, using AI-generated setlists personalized to each group.

3

Caregiver Platform

Expand into a full caregiver tool with Spotify integration, memory archives for families, and partnerships with healthcare organizations. Launch a caregiver dashboard to track engagement and emotional well-being metrics.
