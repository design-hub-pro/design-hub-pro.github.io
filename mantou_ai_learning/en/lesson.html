Generate your first cyber pet 🐾

In this lesson you'll use the prompt pillars from Lesson 05 to tell AI exactly what kind of pet you want — and you'll actually get a creature with a look, a personality, and a voice.

Goal
Write a prompt template that reliably generates your pet
After this
Reuse the template to generate 100+ unique pets

Key concepts

①
Instruction
Tell AI exactly what to do
②
Context
Give it the world / character setup
③
Example
Show 1–2 sample outputs

Steps

  1. 1
    Open the Prompt Editor
    Click "Open Builder" in the top right to enter the prompt lab.
  2. 2
    Fill in your pet's three pillars
    Write its appearance, personality, and special skill, one by one.
  3. 3
    Click Generate and look at the first result
    Notice what's different from your imagination — that gap is your prompt's blind spot.
  4. 4
    Refine and regenerate twice
    Until your pet shows up the way you imagined it, every time.
  5. ✓
    Save the template and move on
Common mistakes
  • Just writing "a really cool pet" — too vague, AI doesn't know what cool means.
  • No example — try adding "for example: a tiny fox in electric armor."
  • Asking for too much at once — get a stable look first, then add abilities.
