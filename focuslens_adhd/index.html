See It Work

Project in Action

Real-world scenarios showing how FocusLens AI supports ADHD students

Real-Time Focus Monitoring

The Chrome extension quietly tracks typing rhythm and mouse patterns to detect when a student's attention begins to drift, providing gentle nudges before full distraction sets in.

Personalized Study Insights

After each session, students can review their focus patterns, identify their peak concentration times, and learn which study strategies work best for them.

Educator Dashboard Support

Teachers and counselors can access anonymized, aggregated focus trends to provide better accommodations and identify students who may need additional support.

Parent-Student Collaboration

Parents gain visibility into study habits without invasive monitoring. Shared reports foster constructive conversations about learning strategies rather than screen-time battles.

Growth Path

From Prototype to Platform

1

Build the Chrome Extension

Develop a privacy-first Chrome extension using Manifest V3 that captures keyboard and mouse event metrics (not content). Start with a small pilot group of 10-20 ADHD students at your school to establish behavioral baselines.

2

Train the AI Model

Use TensorFlow.js to build a lightweight, on-device ML model that learns each student's unique focus patterns. Partner with your school's special education department to validate the model's nudge effectiveness against clinical assessments.

3

Scale to School Districts

Create an educator dashboard showing anonymized, aggregate focus trends. Submit to the Chrome Web Store and present results at education technology conferences. Partner with disability services offices at universities for broader adoption.

Your Journey

From Passion to Purpose

How a student's drive to support neurodivergent learners led to building FocusLens AI

Foundation

"I always wondered why some of my classmates struggled so much with online assignments, even though they were clearly smart. I started reading about ADHD and neurodiversity."

Observation

"During remote learning, I noticed how my friend with ADHD would constantly switch tabs, restart typing, and lose track of assignments. No tool was helping her stay on track."

Insight

"I realized that keyboard and mouse behavior could reveal so much about attention. If we could track these patterns without recording actual content, we could build a non-invasive focus helper."

Innovation

"FocusLens AI became my mission — a Chrome extension that helps ADHD students understand their own focus patterns. It doesn't judge; it empowers. And it keeps every byte of data on their device."
