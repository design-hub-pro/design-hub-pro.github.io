Concept visuals. Every number on this page is a design target, not a measured result.

The Band

An IMU catches the first lateral jolt of a knock. A capacitive grip ring keeps the buzzer quiet when a hand is simply holding or lifting the cup.

Target: chirp approx. 120 ms after the first lateral jolt, before the tip completes

The band alone — approx. 24 g, 30 mm tall, 4 mm wall

Sits on the lower third of the wall, well clear of the drinking lip

Rolls on in about 5 seconds, no tools, one size covers a 25 mm span

Sense to alert in approx. 120 ms; the grip ring gates the buzzer

5 layers inside a 4 mm wall, IP54 silicone over-mould

Servo tilt rig, approx. 7° trip threshold, every run logged

110 mAh cell on a 3.3 V rail, approx. 38 µA idle draw

The Base

A load cell cannot sit in the band, because no part of the cup's weight passes through it. So the weighing moves to a coaster. It reads the cup at rest and hands the number to the band over BLE, which carries it while the cup is lifted and tightens the tip threshold on a full cup.

Tares in approx. 400 ms on set-down; each sip logged as a mass delta

The base alone — 104 mm across, 12 mm tall, USB-C charged

Single-point load cell on a 1 kg flexure, 24-bit ADC

Mass crosses to the band over BLE; a full cup is set to trip approx. 30% earlier
