Last-Mile Food Pantry Routing

Intermediate Food Security Optimization
Brought by World Food Bank · Detroit Pilot

The Real-World Problem

A mobile food pantry serves 14 neighborhoods with perishable produce. The driver only has 6 hours and a 200-mile range. Every minute saved on routing means more families fed before food spoils.

Your Math Mission

Formulate this as a Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem with time windows. Find a tour that maximizes families served while respecting shelf-life constraints. Compare a greedy heuristic to an LP-relaxation lower bound.

Why This Matters

A 15% improvement = ~3,200 more meals delivered each month in Detroit alone. Your solution will be tested in the field next quarter.
