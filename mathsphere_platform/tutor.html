Integration by Parts

The formula comes from the product rule for derivatives:

∫ u dv = uv - ∫ v du

The key is choosing u and dv wisely. Use the LIATE rule as a guide for choosing u (pick the first one that appears):

L Logarithmic functions (ln x, log x)
I Inverse trig functions (arcsin, arctan)
A Algebraic functions (x², x³)
T Trigonometric functions (sin, cos)
E Exponential functions (e^x, 2^x)

Let me show you with an example. Let's solve ∫ x·e^x dx:

Step 1: Choose u = x (algebraic) and dv = e^x dx

Step 2: Then du = dx and v = e^x

Step 3: Apply the formula: x·e^x - ∫ e^x dx

Step 4: = x·e^x - e^x + C = e^x(x - 1) + C
