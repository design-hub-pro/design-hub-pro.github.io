The Problem

The Gap Between Brain Signals and Machine Understanding

Brain-computer interfaces hold immense promise for restoring communication, enabling assistive devices, and supporting mental health — yet they remain limited by poor signal classification accuracy, high individual variability in EEG patterns, and a lack of interpretable AI models that clinicians and researchers can trust. Bridging this gap requires robust, generalizable algorithms that can decode motor imagery signals reliably across diverse users.
