One mission, four decisions

Every run is short enough to replay three times in a class period.

1

Read the map, then commit

Blocked roads, rising water, three shelters, nine people waiting. Students pick a vehicle and draw a route before the clock starts. Hesitating costs seconds, rushing costs safety.

2

Hazards react to you

Take the fast road through low ground and the water may follow. The hazard model adjusts to each choice, so no two runs match.

3

Supplies run out

4 first-aid kits, 2 boats, 1 truck, 30 liters of fuel. Every pickup is a trade-off.

4

See the best route after

A pathfinding replay draws the safest efficient route over the student's own path, with the moment they diverged.

FOR TEACHERS

A drill you can grade

Each run is scored on four things: lives reached, time, safety, and supply use. The class dashboard shows where students keep making the same mistake, like driving through water deeper than 15 cm.
