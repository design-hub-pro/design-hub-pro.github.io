The Koi Fish's Dream

A story about perseverance and transformation

2:15 5:30

Little Koi swam against the river's current every day. The other fish laughed and said, "Why do you try so hard?"

But Koi remembered what Grandmother Turtle had told her: "At the top of the waterfall, a magical gate awaits..."

Cultural Note

In Japanese culture, koi fish symbolize perseverance and courage. The legend says a koi that climbs the Dragon Gate waterfall transforms into a dragon!
