How Tempo Works

One model. Built only for you.

Tempo fuses passive signals and quick daily tests into a personal model that predicts your fatigue curve — and learns more accurate every night.

1 · Track 9 signals

Sleep, bedtime consistency, homework, exercise intensity, screen time, reaction & memory tests, self-ratings, plus optional wearable data.

2 · Train your model

Tempo finds your unique fatigue patterns — what drains you, what recovers you — and retrains nightly to predict tomorrow's energy.

3 · Act ahead of time

Get forecasted crash windows and a concrete plan — nap timing, fuel, study windows, lights-out — to beat fatigue before it lands.
