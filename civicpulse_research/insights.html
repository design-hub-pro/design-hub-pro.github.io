The five stories residents tell

Clustered from 14,820 posts · click a card to see source quotes

CivicPulse groups everything residents said into the 5 recurring narratives below. Size reflects share of total conversation.

32%
"Developers > residents"
4,742 voices · Anger 71%

City keeps approving luxury towers while local families are forced out. Specific projects on Maple & Oak cited.

24%
"Airbnb stole my block"
3,556 voices · Anger 48%

Short-term rentals on Brookline & 9th Ave blamed for vacancies. Asks for a per-host cap and registration.

18%
"The numbers don't add up"
2,668 voices · Resignation 62%

Residents do their own math: median rent vs median wage. Many cite recent census + Zillow data.

15%
"Where do my kids live?"
2,223 voices · Fear 58%

Parents anxious about kids leaving the neighborhood to find housing. Especially strong on YouTube comments.

11%
"Maple Lofts gives hope"
1,631 voices · Hope 64%

The mayor's recent mixed-income project on Maple is cited as a real positive — replicate it widely.
