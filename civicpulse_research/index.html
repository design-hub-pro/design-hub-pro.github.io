before reading the headlines?

CivicPulse listens to Twitter, Reddit, YouTube, TikTok and 311 logs to answer the question every mayor's office actually asks: "What do residents really think about this — right now?" Drop a topic in, and within 6 minutes you get sentiment, narrative clusters, who is talking, where they live, and the concrete asks behind the noise — all redacted, audited, and resident-respectful.

Why CivicPulse · why now

Town halls only hear the room. Polls only hear who answers the phone. CivicPulse hears everyone who's already talking.

The problem

Mayors make daily decisions on housing, transit, schools and public safety using polls that are a quarter old, town halls that are self-selected, and 311 logs that only capture what's broken — never what people feel.

The wedge

Residents are already telling the city exactly what they think — on Reddit, X, YouTube, TikTok. CivicPulse turns that public corpus into clustered, weighted, ethics-redacted intelligence in 6 minutes.

The moat

A defensible city-government product: ethics-board reviewed, PII redacted, 311-correlated, council-presentable. Once a city hall installs CivicPulse, they cannot run a policy launch without it.

CivicPulse is a research showcase prototype · not affiliated with any real city government
