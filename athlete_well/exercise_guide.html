Quad Stretches

Phase 2 • ACL Recovery

Beginner
3 sets × 30 sec Low Impact

Instructions

1

Starting Position

Stand near a wall or chair for balance support.

2

Perform Stretch

Bend your knee and grab your ankle, pulling heel toward glutes gently.

3

Hold & Breathe

Hold for 30 seconds while breathing deeply. Don't bounce.

Pro Tip

Keep your knees close together and hips pushed forward for maximum stretch.

Stop if you feel sharp pain

Mild discomfort is normal, pain is not
