Social Impact

Why Teen Girls Need Better Chemical Safety Tools

Rising Youth Exposure

Teen girls now use an average of 6 skincare products daily with routines exceeding 10 minutes—often using products designed for mature adult skin due to social media influence.

ScienceDaily Research

Surging Contact Allergies

Contact allergies in children have significantly increased over 20 years, with cosmetics now identified as the main cause—early exposure can create lifelong sensitivities.

Finnish Health Registry Study

Gender Gap in Safety Testing

Chemical safety research has historically used adult male models, systematically underestimating exposure differences and health risks for women and adolescents.

TIME Investigation

Clean Beauty Confusion

65% of UK consumers feel they cannot accurately gauge a brand's safety from its own claims. "Natural" and "clean" labels have no regulatory definition in the US.

Growth Path

From Prototype to Platform

1

School Pilot Program

Partner with 2-3 high schools to pilot GlowSafe Pro with student health clubs and chemistry classes. Collect real user feedback on ingredient concerns and usability from diverse teen demographics.

2

Database Partnership

Establish data partnerships with organizations like EWG (Environmental Working Group) and academic toxicology labs. Integrate verified safety data to enhance AI accuracy and scientific credibility.

3

Community Expansion

Launch user-contributed safety reviews and build an online community for teens to share experiences. Partner with dermatologists and chemists to provide expert-verified educational content.

Your Journey

From Passion to Purpose

How Jenny's unique combination of chemistry expertise, leadership experience, and artistic sensibility shapes this project.

Foundation

"My chemistry coursework opened my eyes to how molecules interact with our bodies. When I started reading ingredient labels, I realized most people—including me—had no idea what we were actually putting on our skin."

Observation

"As a leader in CRC and Stage Manager for drama productions, I've seen how decisions made without full information lead to problems. The beauty industry felt the same—marketing 'clean' without clear definitions creates more confusion than clarity."

Insight

"My K-pop training taught me that appearance and self-expression matter—but they shouldn't come at the cost of health. I wanted to create something that celebrates beauty culture while empowering informed choices."

Innovation

"GlowSafe Pro combines everything I care about: chemistry, technology, leadership, and self-expression. It's designed by a teen, for teens—because we deserve tools that take both our health and our identities seriously."
