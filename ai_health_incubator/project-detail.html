MindBridge AI

An AI-powered mental health companion that uses cognitive behavioral therapy techniques and real-time mood analysis to help users manage anxiety and stress.

Stanford University
Started Jan 2024

Problem Statement

Over 40 million adults in the US suffer from anxiety disorders, yet only 36.9% receive treatment. Traditional therapy is often inaccessible due to cost, time, and stigma. There's a critical need for accessible, evidence-based mental health support that can complement professional care.

Our Solution

MindBridge AI provides 24/7 mental health support through an AI companion trained on cognitive behavioral therapy principles. Our system analyzes voice patterns, text inputs, and optional biometric data to detect mood changes and deliver personalized interventions.

AI Conversations

Empathetic chat support using CBT-based dialogue

Mood Tracking

Real-time mood analysis with pattern recognition

Crisis Detection

Automatic escalation to human counselors
