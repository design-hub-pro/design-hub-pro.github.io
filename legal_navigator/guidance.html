Step 1

Law that may apply

Statute

Texas Property Code §92.103–92.109 requires a landlord to refund your deposit or send an itemized list of deductions within 30 days of move-out. Normal wear and tear cannot be charged to you.

"A landlord who in bad faith retains a deposit may be liable for up to 3× the amount plus $100." — §92.109

Step 2

Your rights & responsibilities

You can demand the refund plus a written itemization.

You may recover penalties if the landlord acted in bad faith.

You must have given a forwarding address in writing.
