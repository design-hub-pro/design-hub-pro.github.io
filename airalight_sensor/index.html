The Problem

Indoor Air Pollution Is an Invisible Health Crisis

People spend roughly 90% of their time indoors, yet most have no idea about the quality of the air they breathe. Indoor air can be 2 to 5 times more polluted than outdoor air due to cooking fumes, off-gassing furniture, dust, mold, and poor ventilation. Without visible cues, dangerous pollutant levels go completely unnoticed.

Product Vision

The AiraLight

AiraLight — Where lighting meets environmental intelligence

AiraLight replaces conventional stair lights, wall sconces, and nightlights with a beautiful fixture that continuously senses PM2.5, CO2, VOCs, temperature, and humidity. Instead of burying data in an app, it communicates air quality through the universal language of color — green means clean, yellow means moderate, red means act now.

See It Work

Project in Action

Real-world scenarios where AiraLight keeps you informed about the air you breathe

Kitchen Cooking Alert

While cooking, the stair light shifts from green to yellow as PM2.5 rises from stir-frying. A gentle amber glow reminds you to turn on the range hood — no buzzer needed.

Overnight Bedroom Monitoring

As CO2 accumulates overnight in a closed bedroom, the nightlight gradually shifts color, signaling you to crack a window for fresh air and better sleep quality.

Nursery & Baby Room

Parents can glance at the hallway light to instantly know if the nursery air is safe. VOC detection catches off-gassing from new furniture or cleaning products.

Classroom Air Quality

In a packed classroom, CO2 levels rise quickly. The wall light turns amber, prompting the teacher to open windows — improving student focus and reducing drowsiness.

Growth Path

From Prototype to Product

1

Build & Test at Home

Assemble the sensor array on a breadboard with ESP32-C3, calibrate readings against a reference monitor, and 3D-print the light enclosure. Validate color-mapping accuracy across AQI ranges.

2

Campus Pilot Program

Deploy 10 units across dorm stairwells and classrooms. Collect user feedback on color intuitiveness, alert thresholds, and design preferences. Iterate on form factor and firmware.

3

Smart Home Integration

Release as an open-source Home Assistant integration. Partner with smart home retailers and pitch to property managers for building-wide deployment in apartments and schools.

Community Initiative

BreatheRight Initiative

A student-led non-profit dedicated to raising indoor air quality awareness and providing free monitoring tools to underserved communities.

Our Mission

Clean air shouldn't be a luxury — it should be visible to everyone

To make indoor air quality visible and actionable for every household — especially low-income families, schools in underserved areas, and elderly care homes — through free sensor kits, educational workshops, and community air quality data.

What We Do

Free Sensor Kit Drives

Build and distribute free AiraLight kits to families in communities with high pollution exposure. Each kit includes the sensor, installation guide, and a QR code linking to educational materials.

Quarterly build-a-thon events

School Air Quality Workshops

Partner with K-12 schools to run hands-on workshops where students build their own air sensors, learn about pollutants, and become air quality ambassadors in their homes.

STEM education outreach

Community Air Quality Map

Aggregate anonymized data from deployed AiraLights to create a hyperlocal indoor air quality map, helping identify neighborhoods and building types with the worst indoor air conditions.

Open-source data platform
