Why crash diets stunt growth

Teen bodies are still building bone and height. Cutting too hard slows both, and the weight usually comes back.

General adolescent sports-nutrition guidance
Protein: aim for 1.2–2.0g per kg

That range supports muscle repair and growth for a training teen — below adult bodybuilder doses, above a sedentary diet.

ISSN pediatric & adolescent position stand
Hydration moves the needle on performance

Losing more than 2% of body weight in sweat measurably lowers strength and focus in the same session.

ACSM exercise & fluid replacement guidance
These are your peak bone-building years

Calcium and vitamin D intake during the teen years sets bone density for life — roughly 1,300mg calcium a day is the target for ages 9–18.

NIH Dietary Reference Intake, ages 9–18
Sleep is part of the plan, not extra

Growth hormone release peaks during deep sleep. Teens training hard need 8–10 hours a night to actually recover.
