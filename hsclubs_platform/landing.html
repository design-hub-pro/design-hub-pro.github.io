Features

Everything your school needs

From club discovery to governance oversight, HSClubs covers the entire club lifecycle in one integrated platform.

Club Discovery

Students search and filter clubs by interest, category, meeting day, and availability. No more asking around or digging through Google Docs.

Smart Applications

One-click apply with customizable forms. Leaders review, approve, deny, or waitlist with a single tap. No more lost Google Form responses.

Member Management

Track attendance, assign roles, manage officers, and monitor member engagement all in one dashboard. Real-time member directory.

Announcements & Events

Post updates, create events, and schedule meetings in one place. Push notifications keep everyone informed without Discord chaos.

Activity Records

Students build verifiable records of participation, leadership, and achievements. Export-ready for college applications and resumes.

School Governance

Admins approve new clubs, monitor activity, manage charter renewals, and ensure smooth leadership transitions every year.

How It Works

Up and running in minutes

Getting your school started with HSClubs takes three simple steps.

1

Set Up Your School

An admin creates the school profile, adds branding, and configures club categories and approval workflows.

2

Import or Create Clubs

Club leaders create profiles with descriptions, schedules, requirements, and photos. Bulk import from existing spreadsheets.

3

Invite Students

Share the invite link. Students discover clubs, apply to join, and start building their involvement records instantly.
