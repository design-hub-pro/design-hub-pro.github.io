Why this exists

High school clubs are the biggest unfilled gap in the school stack.

Every school runs 50–150 clubs. They're how students figure out who they are and how admissions readers judge them. Yet they live in Google Docs, parent texts, Discord groups, and one heroic teacher's spreadsheet. HSClubs ends that.

Before

With HSClubs

AI matches students to the right clubs. Activity is verified, points reward engagement, and the college profile builds itself in the background.
