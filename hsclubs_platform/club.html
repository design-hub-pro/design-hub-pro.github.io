Our mission

Engineering as a contact sport.

We don't run a classroom. We run a workshop with whiteboards covered in robot drivetrains, build-season deadlines that don't move, and a 40-page engineering notebook that ships with our hardware.

Members rotate through CAD, electronics, programming, and team strategy. By senior year, you'll have designed a competition-grade module from scratch, debugged it under tournament pressure, and either mentored an underclassman or led a sub-team.

Engineering rigor Real-deadline shipping Mentorship culture Inclusive — no experience required

What we actually do

Four sub-teams. One shipping deadline.

Every member joins one sub-team in their first year and is encouraged to rotate. Sub-teams ship to a shared milestone calendar — no silos.

3-question form + a 1-paragraph "what would you build" prompt. Officers respond within 5 days.

Membership requirements

  • Open to all grade levels (9–12). No prior experience required.
  • Attend at least 1 build night per week during the season (Tue or Thu).
  • Pass the 1-hour lab safety briefing before touching machines.
  • $25 annual dues (fee waivers available — just ask a counselor).
  • Show up. We track attendance because it matters for sub-team continuity.
