About This Club

The Robotics Club is dedicated to exploring the exciting world of robotics, engineering, and programming. Members work together to design, build, and program robots for various competitions including FIRST Robotics and VEX. We welcome students of all skill levels — whether you're a beginner curious about engineering or an experienced coder looking to push boundaries.

Our team placed 3rd at the 2025 State Championship and we're gearing up for an even stronger season. Join us to gain hands-on experience with mechanical design, electronics, programming, and teamwork.
