1Mission Brief

This is not homework. This is a product challenge.

Irvine has 20+ adult-focused tea chains — but none designed specifically for teens. You're going to change that. Here's the brief.

01 · THE GAP
🏙️

Market Opportunity

Brands like Hey Tea and Yi Dian Dian target adults and college students. Middle and high schoolers want something more fun, social, and made for them. That whitespace is yours.

02 · THE CHALLENGE
🎯

Your Mission

Create a brand-new bubble tea brand that feels younger, more playful, more social — and clearly different from every adult-style tea shop out there.

03 · YOUR ROLE
🚀

AI Product Builder

You'll use AI tools to handle everything: brand naming, logo concepts, user research, feature design, app prototyping, and a final launch showcase.

04 · THE OUTPUT
💎

Ship a Real Product

By the end, you'll have a brand kit, a working app prototype, a reward system, social features, and a pitch — ready for demo day.

01
Milestone 01 · Discovery

Understand the Mission

Get inside the head of your real user. Why does a teen-only milk tea brand need to exist? What do middle and high schoolers want that adult chains can't offer?

⏱ ~45 min
🟢 Easy
Not Started

📌What you'll do

1Define your target user (middle school vs. high school students) in 1–2 clear sentences.
2List 3–5 things teens want in a boba brand that adult chains don't offer.
3Pick 3–5 brand keywords that describe the vibe you're going for.
4Write a 2-sentence "brand vibe" description — what should this brand feel like?

✨How AI can help you

Generate teen personas
Spot youth trends
Brainstorm vibe words
Compare adult vs teen brands

💡Try this prompt

AI Prompt HelperPersona generator
Generate a target user profile for a bubble tea brand designed for middle school and high school students in Irvine. The brand should feel youthful, fun, social, colorful, and different from adult-focused tea chains. Include: daily routine, social habits, what makes them buy a drink, and what would make them tell 5 friends about a new tea brand.
💡 Builder Tip: Don't just write "teens like fun stuff." Get specific — what app do they open first thing in the morning? What flavor would they post on their story? The more specific, the better your brand will be.

02
Milestone 02 · Brand Identity

Brand Naming & Identity

The moment you name your brand, it gets real. Brainstorm at least 10, pick your top 3, lock 1, then design the slogan, personality, and logo concept.

⏱ ~75 min
🟢 Easy
Locked

📌What you'll do

1Brainstorm at least 10 brand name candidates with AI.
2Shortlist your top 3 and explain why each works.
3Lock the final brand name.
4Write a slogan / tagline.
5Describe brand personality in 3–5 words.
6Describe or generate a logo concept.

💡Name brainstorm prompt

AI Prompt HelperBrand name generator
Generate 15 milk tea brand names for a new bubble tea brand targeting middle and high school students. The brand should feel youthful, playful, social, trendy, and different from adult-focused tea shops. Mix English and creative invented words. Each name should be 1–2 words and easy to say out loud.

🎨Logo concept prompt

AI Image PromptFor Midjourney / DALL·E
A modern, playful logo for a teen milk tea brand called [YOUR NAME]. Mascot-style, pastel peach + cream + matcha green palette, soft rounded shapes, bubble tea pearls integrated, sticker-friendly, gen-z packaging vibes, on white background.
💡 Builder Tip: Test your final name out loud to a real friend. If they smile or repeat it back immediately, you've got a winner. If they say "wait what?", iterate.

03
Milestone 03 · Product Strategy

Feature Planning

Now decide what your app actually does. Menu, rewards, social — those are table stakes. Your job: make each one feel teen-native, not boring.

⏱ ~60 min
🟡 Medium
Locked

📌What you'll do

1List every feature your app could include (aim for 10+).
2Pick your 3 core features and explain why each matters to teens.
3Pick 2 bonus features that make your app unique.
4Design the reward system — what do teens earn, and how do they spend it?
5Design the social feature — why would a teen share this with friends?

🎯Required feature pillars

Menu & Ordering
Reward / Loyalty System
Social / Sharing Hook
Personalization

💡Try this prompt

AI Prompt HelperFeature ideation
Create a feature list for a milk tea app designed for middle school and high school students. The app should include rewards, social features, and a more playful student-focused experience than adult bubble tea shop apps. Group features into: Ordering, Rewards, Social, Personalization. For each feature, explain in 1 sentence why a teen would actually use it daily.
💡 Builder Tip: A good reward system is one a teen can explain to their friend in 10 seconds flat. If you need a paragraph to explain it — simplify.

Milestone 04 · Architecture

App Structure & User Flow

Map every screen and every tap. From "open the app" to "share the order with friends" — design the path so it feels effortless.

⏱ ~60 min
🟡 Medium
Locked

📌What you'll do

1List your 5–7 main app pages.
2Design the user flow — what happens when a user taps through your app?
3Write 1 detailed user journey: a teen opening the app, ordering, sharing, and coming back tomorrow.

📱Suggested pages

Home / Feed
Menu / Order
Rewards / Loyalty
Social / Friends
Profile
Specials / Drops
Drink Builder / Customizer

💡Try this prompt

AI Prompt HelperUser flow design
I'm designing a milk tea app for teens called [YOUR BRAND]. The app has these pages: Home, Menu, Rewards, Social, Profile, and Specials. Create a detailed user flow showing how a 15-year-old student opens the app after school, browses the menu, customizes a drink, earns rewards, and shares their order with friends. Show each screen transition with arrows.
💡 Builder Tip: Great apps feel like the user already knows what to do — before reading a single word. If your flow needs a tutorial, simplify it.

Milestone 07 · Launch

Launch & Showcase

Turn your build into a story. Write the launch headline, explain what makes you different, and deliver a 1-minute pitch like you're on stage at demo day.

⏱ ~75 min
🟠 Hard
Locked

📌What you'll do

1Write a launch title and tagline that hooks people instantly.
2Write a 1-paragraph product summary — what is this app, and who is it for?
3Write a "brand difference" statement — how is your app different from regular boba shop apps?
4Write or record a 1-minute showcase script — your demo day pitch.

💡Try this prompt

AI Prompt HelperLaunch description
Write a short launch description for a new bubble tea app designed for middle and high school students. Highlight what makes it more social, fun, and youth-focused than regular tea shop apps. The tone should be exciting and confident, like a startup launch announcement.
💡 Builder Tip: Your pitch should pass the "hallway test" — if you explained it to someone in a hallway in under 60 seconds, would they say "that sounds cool"?
